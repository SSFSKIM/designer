**The transmission, as the renderer computes it, and where a per-pixel alpha enters.**
`wgsl/optics.ts` computes, per pixel, `sizedAlpha = tintAlpha + sizeOcclusionGain · sizeK(span) ·
(1 − tintAlpha)` with `sizeK = smoothstep(sizeSpanMin, sizeSpanMax, span)` from the pixel's span in
the field pass's aux target, then runs the W9 solve at that `sizedAlpha` against the group's
linear mean (W46 Grounding, the last paragraph; X61): the ordinates are composite-level targets and
the solve holds them at any alpha wherever the shifted neutral is unclamped and the authority is
full. `sizeOcclusionGain` is therefore already a span law on the alpha, from `sizeSpanMin` 32 to
the knee at 96 and flat above it, mirrored per surface by the CSS tier
(`platform-web/src/optics.ts`, `sizeOcclusionGain`), and it cannot separate 96 from 128–160 for
the same reason W45 named for the share: `sizeK` saturates at the knee. The far curve
`farS = smoothstep(sizeSpanMax, sizeScatterSpanMax(dpr), span)` is the span statistic the pass
already reads per pixel for the ramp's far start, the 2x heavy gain and W45's share; at the
inherited top 256 it reads 0.104 at span 128 and 0.352 at 160, and at the light 2x top 128 it reads
1 at 128. The W46 clamps that bound the alpha from below are at `tintAlpha` ≈ 0.63 (0/255 checker,
span ≥ 96), 0.77 (`light-solid`, span ≥ 96) and 0.48 (`light-solid`, span 32).

**The body sample, and where a receded narrow blur enters.** The interior is `mix(bodySample,
scatterColour, kScatter)`: `bodySample` is the source blurred at the body width
(`optics.regular.blurSigma`, a device-px quantity, one per source), `scatterColour` the deep
sample (the chain level `scatterLod`, or the heavy texture where `sizeHeavyTapSigma` is named, with
W30's second tap mixed in by the share W45 grades), and `kScatter` the deep share graded by the span
(`kDeep`) and ramped in depth from the edge by the ramp starts. The body's weight is `1 − kScatter`,
and the shader keeps `sDeep = 1 − kDeep` at every depth, the ramp only raising it toward its start
(`wgsl/optics.ts`, `sharpShare`), before W30's conditioning term lowers `kScatter` by
2·(stat − 0.03) on a source whose edge-density statistic exceeds 0.03 (the dark gain −2, which the
receded difference inherits). At 2x the receded document's deep is the σ 14 CSS px heavy texture
and `sizeScatterFloor2x` is 1, so `sDeep` is 0 and the body is shown only by the ramp (its start at
span 96 is 0.04) and by the conditioning term. At 1x the floor is 0.34 (0.5 at point A), the
receded thick share adds 0.25 and the heavy tap is off, so on a span-96 surface `kDeep` is 0.72 and
the body keeps `sDeep` 0.28 at every depth (the ramp start there is 0.30, so the ramp adds at most
0.02), before the conditioning term; at span 160 `sDeep` is 0.01. The ramp's reach is 80 CSS px at
1x and 50 at 2x (`sizeScatterRampReach1xPx` / `…2xPx`, 80 / 100 device px) against a half-span of
48 on `rrect-md` and 80 on `rrect-lg`, so the span-96 cell has no interior beyond the ramp at either
scale and the span-160 cell has one only at 2x. Where the receded render's 4–8 px structure comes
from is therefore not readable from position; G0 reads it by re-render (Design "The ladders", the
depth-split diagnostic). The pyramid builds a blurred texture of any width through
`heavyTapPlan` (the deepest chain level at or below the width, plus a residual separable Gaussian),
so a narrow tap at 2–4 CSS px is level 0 and a residual: no new pass shape, one more texture where
the share is non-zero.

## Design (advisory unless marked)

### Operator 1 — the span-graded transmission (MARKED; Decision Log 2)

Two leaves, `tintAlphaFar1x` and `tintAlphaFar2x`, identity 0, resolved by `rampAtScale` (held at
the 1x value at dpr ≤ 1, half-way at 1.5, the 2x value at ≥ 2) as every scale-anchored span leaf
is, evaluated per pixel in the optics pass:

```
farS(px)      = smoothstep(sizeSpanMax, sizeScatterSpanMax(dpr), span(px))       // in hand
alphaBase(px) = clamp(tintAlpha + tintAlphaFarAtScale · farS(px), 0, 1)           // NEW
sizedAlpha    = alphaBase + sizeOcclusionGain · sizeK(px) · (1 − alphaBase)        // existing form
```

- **Why this law.** The thin body wants about 0.7, the knee about 0.8 and the thick spans 0.9 or
  above (Purpose). Three knots: `tintAlpha` is the thin value, `sizeOcclusionGain` lifts it to the
  knee on `sizeK` (at span 44 `sizeK` is 0.092, so the thin cells barely see the gain), and the far
  delta lifts it from the knee to the top on `farS`. With `tintAlpha` 0.7 and the gain 0.4 the
  alpha at 96 is 0.82; with the far delta 0.38 at the inherited top 256 the alpha at 160 is 0.90,
  and with the top at 128 the same alpha needs 0.13. The law reuses the two span statistics the
  pass already has and adds one multiply-add and a clamp.
- **Why it holds the level.** The solve runs at the per-pixel alpha (Grounding), so the level holds
  by construction wherever the alpha sits above W46's clamps: the mid and thick alphas (0.82 and
  above) are above the 0/255 checker's 0.63 and `light-solid`'s 0.77; the thin alpha 0.7 is above
  the span-32 clamps (0.57 / 0.48). What the solve does not hold — the authority dip between
  black and the dark anchor, the collapse — L1 and the level check measure at every rung, as W46's
  X61 says.
- **Why the clamp.** The alpha is a mixing weight and the solve divides by it (`shift = … /
  sizedAlpha`), so the sum is clamped to [0, 1]. At identity the expression is `tintAlpha +
  0 · farS`, which is `tintAlpha` exactly in f32, and `clamp(a, 0, 1)` is the identity on an alpha
  already in [0, 1], so the inert leaf changes no raster (clause 1 proves it by bytes).
- **Why two leaves.** The scales' thick overshoots differ (coarse C ×1.26 at 1x against ×1.66 at
  2x; Purpose), the far curve's top is per scale, and every span leaf of W30's set is a 1x/2x pair
  resolved by `rampAtScale`; an implicit 1x zero (W45's choice for a 2x-only effect) would leave
  the 1x thick cells where W46 found them.
- **Where it is read, and where not.** Before the occlusion term and the solve, at the one site the
  pass computes `sizedAlpha`; every other reader of `ou.tint.w` (the collapse's alpha, the presence)
  is listed by G0 and left reading the ungraded alpha unless its reading says otherwise, with the
  reason recorded. The uniform rides a free slot beside W45's (`passes.ts` `d[131]`, 0 today).
- **The CSS tier MIRRORS it** (Decision Log 2): its `rgba()` alpha is one per surface and the
  surface's span is one number, so `platform-web/src/optics.ts` evaluates the same law once per
  surface — `clamp(tintAlpha + rampAtScale(far1x, far2x, dpr) · smoothstep(sizeSpanMax,
  sizeScatterSpanMax(dpr), span), 0, 1)` — before its existing `sizeOcclusionGain` term, and
  `tier-coherence.test.ts` pins the mirror against the shipped documents' values (0 everywhere
  until a dark document names it). This is the first of W30's spanning set the CSS tier mirrors
  rather than declines since the σ law, because it is a scalar per surface.
- **Identity.** Two plain value drops in `MATERIAL_IDENTITY_TABLE` (no gate leaf: the expression
  is read at every alpha), pinned by `w31-identity-table.test.ts`.
- **Domain and grid** (part 1): `tintAlphaFar1x`, `tintAlphaFar2x` ∈ [0, 0.6], grid {0, 0.1, 0.2,
  0.3, 0.45, 0.6}; `tintAlpha` {0.9, 0.8, 0.7} active (W46's passing rungs); `sizeOcclusionGain`
