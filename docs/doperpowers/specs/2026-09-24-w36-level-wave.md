## Purpose

W35 G0a (§5.177) read, on W34's uniform greys and chromatic solids, that vitrea's deep body — well
inside the edge, where no rim, lens or scatter acts — misses Apple's LEVEL by more than the edge
line it was looking for: **+49 codes on light black, +28 on dark black**, +3…+8 on light mid-greys,
−1…−7 on dark mid-greys, and **up to 111 (active) and 144 (inactive) codes on saturated channels**.
The memo found three mechanisms, not one wrong curve:

1. **Black is a fallback, not a curve point.** The tone solve's authority is
   `smoothstep(anchor/2, anchor, x)` with the first knot's abscissa 0.004 (`wgsl/optics.ts` ~1168;
   mirrored on the CSS tier at `platform-web/src/optics.ts` ~616). At a pure-black backdrop it is
   exactly 0, the solve is off, and the body falls back to `sizedAlpha · neutral` — **181 codes on
   light, 60 on dark**, which is the web reading to the code and the whole of the black miss. The
   fade was never a fitted law; the first knot was fitted on `impulse` (a structured backdrop) or
   floored at 0 (the dark receded document, whose unexplained +0.0475 floor in §5.154 §6 is, by
   arithmetic, probably this same fallback). Restoring authority alone would render −4 / −20 / +21 /
   −20 codes against native (light active / light inactive / dark active / dark inactive): the knot
   itself must be fitted on uniform black.
2. **The curve's middle sits where `photo` put it.** The shipped response reproduces all 48
   non-black web medians on the bed within 0.5 code — the web body's luma IS the curve — and its
   third knot (0.425, fitted on `photo`, a chromatic structured backdrop, W29 G3) sits +0.05 linear
   above Apple's uniform-grey curve at the same input. Structure alone barely moves Apple's level
   (the neutral checkerboard reads within 1–3 codes of grey-128), so the excess is either photo's
   chroma coupling into luma or a knot in the wrong place; the greys decide.
3. **The chromatic miss is a chroma SCALE, pose-independent on Apple's side.** Apple keeps the
   backdrop's linear chromaticity whole on the dark scheme (implied retention 0.95–1.03) and at
   0.86–0.93 on the unclipped light cells, the same in both poses and holding hue within 0–2°;
   vitrea's implied retention is 0.17–0.60, differs by pose, and its mixing toward grey at low
   retention is what rotates hue. The receded pose's abscissa (the silhouette's per-channel
   encoded sum, W28) reads saturated colours about 0.13 low and compounds the dark inactive misses.
   Two limits bound what can be identified: light red, blue and magenta, dark-active blue and every
   macOS 27 `mid-chroma-solid` cell are **censored at 255**, and on the dark scheme even full
   retention leaves Apple's chromatic cells **5–28 codes brighter** than any luminance-keyed curve
   predicts — a form nobody has identified. And the likeliest stop: the solids want retention
   0.9–1.0, which at M1's measured slopes (§5.164 §4) pushes its chroma-to-structure ratio to about
   2.8 (light) and 4.9 (dark), far outside [0.8, 1.2]; M1 reads chroma VARIATION over `photo`, the
   solids read MEAN chroma, so the missing operator may act on the plate's mean colour rather than
   being more per-pixel retention.

Scale and shape drop out (all 104 solid cells read identical medians at 1x and 2x; every span-44
geometry agrees within a code; the thick row rests on the canonical `rrect-md` solids and W34's
`continuous-160`). The frozen macOS 26.5 documents share the chroma defect (their receded
`mid-chroma-solid` misses −67 / +16 / −79 on dark) and cannot be checked at black; they do not move.

The purpose is Apple's body level on macOS 27 — at black, across the greys, and in chroma — closed on
the WebGPU tier under every stop the ledger carries with a level row of its own adopted, the CSS tier
