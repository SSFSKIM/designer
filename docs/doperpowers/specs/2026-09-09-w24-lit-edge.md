## Purpose

**The lit edge.** W23 gave every surface a rim of the right amount, read on the straight span of
every side. The user's eye read the result as drawn: Apple's edge on a dark capsule is not constant
around the shape, and ours is. Read along the whole contour (`results/2026-09-09-w24-lit-edge/
finding/angular-read.txt`), the reference's rim is LIT: on the 2x dark `dark-solid__rrect-md` the
top-left and bottom-right corners read 0.041 / 0.042, the top-right and bottom-left 0.006, and the
four straight sides 0.032–0.033; on the 2x dark capsule the arcs run 0.007 → 0.031 while the
straight spans sit at 0.026; the light `dark-solid__rrect-md` reads tl 0.279 / br 0.282 against tr
0.069 / bl 0.068 with the sides at 0.24–0.25. That is `|n · L|` — or a root of it — with L on the
top-left ↔ bottom-right diagonal, symmetric at both ends, over a small ambient floor. vitrea's rim
reads the same number wherever the normal is horizontal or vertical (0.036 / 0.32) and a little
less on the arcs: a drawn line.

No instrument in three waves could see it, and the reason is exact: a light on the 45° diagonal
projects equally on all four straight sides, so every per-side reader — W21's band peak, W23's
contour span — reads a flat rim with `L−R` = `T−B` = 0, which is also what the reference reads
there. The variation lives in the corner arcs, which the contour reader excludes by construction
and the band reader averages into what W23 clause 4 called the corner overshoot. W22's specular
(a one-sided Lambert from `lightDirection` [−0.37, −0.93], 22° off vertical) was the wrong shape and
was rightly fitted to 0; the right shape is a symmetric cosine about the diagonal.

**The collapse that keeps transmission.** The impulse background is fifteen white dots on black.
Through the collapsed capsule the reference passes the centre dot — a peak four times the body,
4 CSS px wide at 2x — and vitrea passes nothing: its collapse pulls the material onto the backdrop's
MEAN (W7's "texture collapse", measured on a solid backdrop where there was no texture to keep),
where the reference's collapsed material is a dark glass that still transmits what is beneath it,
blurred. The uncollapsed thick cell over the same dots passes them in both. This is the term W21
and W22 carried as "the appearance switch" and W23 G0 measured at −16 / −19 codes on the dark
`checkerboard` / `photo` capsules, seen on the cleanest cell the bed has for it.

This wave reads the rim around the whole contour on both beds, fits the lit edge's law on the
corners and arcs where it is legible, gives the collapse a transmission it keeps, lands both on the
GPU tier with the CSS tier deriving what it can, and puts the dark capsule in front of the user's
eye at a zoom where the arcs are visible.

## Design (advisory unless marked)

**The angular instrument (binding).** For each cell, the boundary of the declared shape is
parametrised (capsule: two straight spans and two semicircles; rrect: four spans and four quarter
arcs); at each of ≥ 720 points the peak excess over the body along the inward normal (from 1 px
outside to 4 CSS px inside) is read; the profile is reported binned by the normal's angle (16 bins
of 22.5°) and by segment, native against web, with the ratio of the brightest to the dimmest bin.
Validated by injection (a synthetic rim of known angular profile) and against the parent's
`angular-read.py`. W23's contour span read is reported beside it on the straight spans.

**The lit edge's law (advisory; G0 decides the form).** The rim's amplitude law of W23 stays as the
edge's TOTAL; a symmetric directional factor modulates it around the contour:
`rim = amplitude × (a + (1 − a) × |dot(normal, L)|^p)`, with `L` on the diagonal (the reference's
brightest bins are the top-left and bottom-right corners, equal to 0.001 — so `L` = (−1, −1)/√2
or its negative, one constant for the angle, expected 45°), `a` the ambient floor as a fraction
(dark ~0.15 of peak, light ~0.25 — per scheme unless the rows say one), and `p` the exponent (the
straight sides at 0.75–0.85 of the peak say p ≈ 0.5–0.7 rather than 1). This replaces the one-sided
`spec` term (`specularPower` / `specularGain` / `lightDirection` on the rim retire, or `lightDirection`
becomes the axis and the exponent takes `specularPower`'s slot — G0 chooses the seam that keeps the
inner shadow's light where it is). The amplitude constants of W23 were fitted on the straight
spans, which sit at `a + (1 − a) × 0.707^p` of the peak; when the factor lands, the amplitude is
re-expressed so the straight spans are unchanged (W23's clause 2 holds by construction) and the
corners move. Fitted on the corner and arc bins of the solid cells in both schemes, checked on the
structured cells' arcs.

**The collapse that keeps transmission (advisory; G1 decides the mechanism).** Under collapse the
material's tint is pulled toward the group's mean backdrop colour; the reference's collapsed
material is pulled toward the BLURRED BACKDROP BENEATH THE PIXEL — the level collapses, the
structure passes. The candidate: the collapse's target becomes the per-pixel blurred backdrop
sample (the material's own scatter/blur already computes it for the refraction path) with the
group mean retained only as the tone axis's argument; a constant `collapseTransmission` ∈ [0, 1]
lerps the target between the mean (0, today) and the sample (1), fitted on the impulse capsules'
dot (peak, FWHM) with the dark structured thin cells as the check. The blur under collapse is the
material's own (`blurSigma` 1.25 + the scatter facet); if the dot's FWHM (8 at 1x, 4 at 2x — a
width that does not double with the scale, so it is not a CSS-px blur alone) asks for a different
kernel, that is a finding, not a fit. The alpha solve ("an adapting material stops transmitting",
`solvedAlpha`) is read beside: it may be what removed the transmission, and it stands down where
the collapse owns the pixel. The CSS tier's collapse (`k` in `adaptedSourceOptics`) mirrors what
its one `backdrop-filter` can carry.

**What ships.** The rim's directional factor and its constants in `material.ts` with rationale;
the collapse's target; the CSS mirror's reach recorded; the profile documents re-recorded; the
goldens under `W24_HASHES`; `adopted-thresholds` re-derived at G3.
