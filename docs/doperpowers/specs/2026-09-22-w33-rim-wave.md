## Purpose

After W32 the shadow's falloff matches Apple's per span (C1, twelve of twelve) and what the eye
still sees at a surface's edge is two things the shadow wave was forbidden to touch (W32 X3):

1. **The exterior's black floor.** Over a backdrop pixel Apple renders as exactly (0, 0, 0), the
   WebGPU tier renders (1, 1, 1) — never more — on 35.3 % of the native-black exterior pixels at
   span 160 on 1x light (3,334 of 9,440), 12.5 % at 128, 0 % at 44 (W32 Deferred 2; claims §5.169
   §5). The grounding memo settled its cause off disk: **Apple's macOS 27 material has no lift over
   black at all** — 0 of 67 light cells and 0 of 50 dark cells nonzero over 2,646,074 backdrop-black
   exterior pixels ≥ 2 CSS px out — while Apple's macOS 26.5 material does (21 of 69 and 18 of 56
   cells, up to a native mean of 19.9 bytes on `hc-text-28`). The fitted `liftAmplitude` (0.001
   light / 0.0005 dark) is the lit pixels' degeneracy (§5.62 §4: the shadow's and the lift's shapes
   correlate 0.9998) leaking into a leaf the black pixels determine at 0, and sRGB's slope of 12.92
   at the origin (`wgsl/optics.ts:771`, stored to `rgba8unorm`) is what turns a 5 × 10⁻⁴ linear
   addition into one whole code. The receded pose, whose documents hold `liftAmplitude: 0`, reads
   0.0 % on all four macOS 27 beds. **Gap (A) is a leaf deletion on the four macOS 27 documents with
   a compensating move on the three thick anchors (≈ 2.4 % of the shadow on lit pixels), not a fit.**
2. **Apple's contour stroke.** Apple draws one DEVICE pixel of dark stroke at the contour, on both
   poses, and vitrea draws none (W32 Deferred 3, re-chartered at G2's review closure; §5.169 §10
   B2). The memo's model-free notch statistic (`native[d=0] − min(native[d=±1])` on an exact
   rounded-rect SDF) reads a median of −19.78 bytes with 46 of 51 active cells below −5 on 1x light,
   −21.30 / 31 of 37 inactive, −13.74 / 31 of 40 on 1x dark; **on the frozen macOS 26.5 bed +2.68
   with 1 of 68**, and vitrea's own notch tracks 26.5's to a byte (+1.81), which rules out the
   estimator. It is one device pixel at 1x AND 2x (notch −37.04 vs −35.28 on `light-solid__rrect-md`,
   with `d=+1` at 2x already back at the shadow). §5.166 §7 pins its form: darker than both backdrop
   and body over a light square and **lighter than the backdrop over a dark one, which a
   transmission cannot be** (156 over 255; 0–6 over 0). **Gap (B) is a macOS 27 addition and a NEW
   leaf** — a signed, opacity-blended dark stroke of declared device-pixel width at the contour, in
   the family `strongBorderRim`'s signed `rimAlpha: −3.5` already exercises — absent from the frozen
   26.5 material, so both frozen documents want it at its identity.

The purpose is the two exterior terms closed on the macOS 27 material with every stop W32 adopted

## Design (advisory unless marked)

**(A) is a declaration.** The four macOS 27 documents write `liftAmplitude: 0` as a declared reading
with the measurement beside it; `liftSpanMin` and the lift's shape leaves stay (inert at zero
amplitude, recorded as unread). The anchors' compensation is the same closed-form window solve W32

uniformly black backdrop and fails on a structured one, because the term's source is the σ-40
blurred chain at the pixel's own position (`optics.ts:585–589`); §5.65's S6 note said so in 2026-09
and was never followed.

to settle to the surrounding convention; the UNIT is the parent's (Decision Log 1 (c)): a device-px
width named as such, not a CSS-px pair (1.0 / 0.5) that encodes the same fact twice and drifts at
dpr 3. Signed, opacity-blended, applied at the contour on both poses with no policy asserted
(`strongBorderRim` is policy-substituted and replaces the variant rim wholesale — the wrong shape);
the WebGPU tier has the signed distance at `wgsl/optics.ts:362` and `rimLight` already composites a
signed amplitude. The receded documents carry their own value (on the inactive pose the stroke is
Apple's entire exterior: body 188, first exterior row 156 over 255). The accessibility fold's effect
on the stroke is declared by G0 (does the fold replace it, as it replaces the rim? — decide, do not
inherit) and the two accessibility documents' values are read, not fitted (W32 Deferred 10 owns their
shadow amplitudes; the stroke on those beds is recorded at whatever the fold gives).

**The compositing form is the wave's first real decision** (clause 2). Three candidates G0 tables:
