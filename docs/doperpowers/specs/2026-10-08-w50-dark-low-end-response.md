not disappear. This defect affects both the clearer 0.25 material and the shipped 0.5 default.

## Attribution: one wrong low-end target, multiplied by two different authority regimes

The source is `packages/renderer-webgpu/src/wgsl/optics.ts`, around the W47/W49b alpha law and W9
solve; the CPU mirror is `packages/platform-web/src/optics.ts` (`backdropToneResponseLevel`,
`toneRespondedSourceOptics`, `spanGradedTintAlpha`). The silhouette argument is reduced in
`renderer-webgpu/src/wgsl/silhouette-tone.ts` and read back by `src/silhouette-tone.ts`.
`attribution.json` reproduces W49b's table on the current source and stores these inputs, rather
than pretending the old captures were rendered by newly added inert code.

Let `x` be the packed encoded-luma argument, `mu` the linear mean, `a` the sized transmission
alpha, `A` the response authority, `k` the collapse weight, and `R` the linear target. With full
measured-tone strength, the solve does

```
preCollapse = (R - k*mu) / (1-k)
nominal = (1-a)*mu + a*luma(neutral)
solvedNeutral = clamp(neutral + A*(preCollapse-nominal)/a, 0, 1)
```

For these thick dark rows, k=0. At A=1 the required neutral is
`(R-(1-a)*mu)/a`. A negative result clamps to0. On a sampled-black far-field pixel, the body then
contributes0 even though the group's target R is positive. This is not a broken black anchor.

The ordinary first x knot is0.004 (1.02 encoded codes). Authority is
`smoothstep(0.002,0.004,x)`. W36's separate black branch restores authority and blends toward the
32/20-code target **only below x=0.003** (0.765 code). It ends before these recorded inputs.

**current receded is0.8→0.9, delta0.10**, not W48's old0.20. Size occlusion then gives

Both means matter: for the black/white impulse, `x` is the mean encoded value but `mu` is the mean
linear value, numerically similar because the dots are binary. `decode(x)` is NOT `mu` there.
Substituting it would erase the very floor that makes the negative-neutral mechanism visible.

coefficient. Evaluate the old law at the **actual span**, including its nonlinear size/level

### Why not the other routes?

- **Move only `backdropToneBlackThick`:** zero reach at the recorded impulse inputs and on28.
- **Raise the first ordinary receded ordinate alone:** can remove the clamp but retains the
  authority trough atml, the active overshoot and the wrong second ordinate; its Hermite slope
  also perturbs the following interval. Compact support gives a stated boundary to the repair.
- **Re-knot/refit the existing four-anchor curve:** a genuinely simpler implementation than a new
  branch, but moving its low anchors changes Hermite slopes into the already fitted middle, up to
  the next unchanged anchor, and loses the exact-above64 boundary. Prefer that route only if it can
  meet the same protected-domain contract and full gate without broader tone movement. The compact
  branch is recommended to isolate this repair; no extra experiment or scope is declared here.
- **Authority limited to avoid negative neutral:** prevents a clamp, but picks an arbitrary old
  neutral mixture instead of Apple's response; ml already demonstrates that accidental rescue.
- **Blanket positive output floor/offset:** can repair the vanished black body while leaving
  dark-solid15 codes low, and worsens active's opposite-sign error and the mid-dark residual.
- **Span-only black term:** insufficient at nonzero input and not identified at160/224. The
  proposed span rows are supported by explicit new measurements, not a fitted compensation for
  different x/authority atml andlg.
- **Reuse W42 native T or revive LT:** no1–8 controls, extrapolated thick low end and a previously
  rejected spatial model. Its data informs this declaration; its completion is not a referee.
