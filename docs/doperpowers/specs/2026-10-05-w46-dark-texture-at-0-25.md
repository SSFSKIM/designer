## Purpose

**What the wave is for.** The least possible gap to Apple's material on the dark scheme at the
clearer glass, which the grounding read found to be the widest WebGPU gap the bed carries: 124 of
144 non-holdout T1 cells miss on dark 0.25 (S 85.85 against 26.65 for 1x light 0.25 and 6.11 for
the per-span tap width W45 deferred), the same shape as the shipped-default dark 0.5 (106 of 128;
230 of 272 over both positions). The shape is one thing seen three ways:

1. **The photo body is flat.** P reads ×0.25–0.48 of Apple's interior structure in both poses,
   at both scales, on every photo cell (9 of 9 miss per scale; the holdout's four photo cells
   miss too). The tracker has named its cause since W43 G3: the dark `optics.regular.tintAlpha`
   0.9 transmits a tenth of the backdrop, and W43 Decision Log 7 item 3 held it at its 0.5 value
   while the dark scatter probes (c01d, c01g) moved checkers and not photo.
2. **The rest checkers are under-structured.** C rest reads ×0.54–0.76 (38 of 44 miss per
   scale), the thin cells worst: `checkerboard-64__capsule-button__rest` ×0.25, `checkerboard-32`
   capsule ×0.35, `checkerboard-4` capsule ×0.23–0.33; `hc-text-7__rrect-sm__rest` ×0.13 at 2x.
   On text the eye sees it as a grey slab where Apple shows the bar (tracker, `hc-text-28`).
3. **The receded fine body is over-structured.** F inactive reads ×2.9–3.6
   (`checkerboard-8__rrect-lg__inactive` 0.0237 against 0.0071; `rrect-md` ×2.9–3.3), C inactive
   ×1.1–2.0, the same over-structure W45 narrowed on the light receded document from ×2.1–2.2.

The dark 0.25 documents differ from the dark 0.5 ones in two tone ordinates only
(`backdropToneResponseThick` knots 2–3 active, knot 3 receded), so this is a 0.5-era material at a
position where Apple's own body barely moves below span 96 (W43 G2) and the slider's dark effect
is the MaxLuma cap relaxing at s ≥ 80 (Memo F). What W46 fits is therefore the dark material's
structure as such; the position is where it may move without a ruling on X41.

**The best version of this is three things:**
1. **Declared targets, each with its family and its prediction, and the ladders before the fit.**
   The three targets share leaves (the transmission reaches checkers and photo alike, which is
   why W43's scatter-only probes found no net gain), so the declaration states per target which
   existing leaves are predicted to move it, by how much, from the rows, and G0's ladders read
   each lever on the renders before part 2 is hashed. A lever that moves nothing is struck; a
   target no lever reaches is named with the operator's shape and not fitted (Decision Log 4).
2. **The level held by construction while the transmission opens.** L1 passes on dark 0.25 with
   0.049 of its 0.055 headroom used and a growth bound of 0.005; a `tintAlpha` move that is not
   compensated fails it on every uniform cell. So the transmission family is a JOINT move:
   `tintAlpha` with the tone ordinates (thin, thick and the black branch) re-solved in closed form
   on the calibration uniform cells to the same means, so that structure opens and the level does
   not move except by the solve's own residual, which L1 then reads.
3. **The same rule, the same referees' pattern, read once.** W45's growth-only landing rule, a
   W46-owned referee manifest of the dark probe cells declared before part 1, the canonical dark
   holdout read once at the new bytes (read 8), publication through W40's publisher superseding
   `d0219cd684bf`, T1 adopted for the dark profiles at G2 with every miss named.

**What the wave does not do.** No native capture. No light change and no 0.5 change (X60, X41).
No change to T1's statistic, bar or arithmetic. No pre-chartered operator. No re-read of any
holdout at the current bytes. No ruling on dark 0.5 (the user's; Decision Log 1).

**The arithmetic of the transmission, as the shader's mix implies it.** The body is
`mix(backdrop_scattered, tint, tintAlpha)` with `tint` the tone-adapted colour: at full tone
strength the tint equals the sampled backdrop luminance mapped through the tone curve
(`backdropToneResponseThin/Thick` on `backdropToneAnchorX`; the black branch below 0.003), so on
a uniform backdrop `b` the mean is `(1 − a)·b + a·T(b)` and on a structured one the interior
structure is roughly `(1 − a)·s_scatter + a·T′(b)·s_tone`, where `s_scatter` is the scatter's
pass at the pitch and `s_tone` the structure the tone's own sampling sees (per pixel in the
active document; the silhouette mean in the receded one, where the second term is 0). With
a = 0.9 the direct term is a tenth, which is why photo reads ×0.25–0.48 and why the receded photo
body, whose tone term is flat, reads ×0.25 in both scales. Holding the mean when `a` moves is one
equation per anchor: `T′(b) = (m(b) − (1 − a′)·b) / a′`, solved on the calibration uniform cells
at every rung; the prediction for P follows from the direct term alone.

### The landing rule (MARKED; Decision Log 3)

`d0219cd684bf` (both scales, WebGPU); `B = max(1 code, 2·bar)` with the bar 0.5 code; the
population is the 77 T1 cells per profile (72 at the gate less the referees, the referees and the
five holdout cells added at the exposure); T reads T1-fine and T1-low as W45 does, from a dark
T-band fixture read at the gate; the gated groups are those with three or more gate cells
(F rest, C rest, C inactive, P rest, P inactive, T rest; F inactive with three probe cells less a
referee is REPORTED, as is T inactive). The budget — at most three cells `away` with `g > B`, none
past `3B`, over the whole population — is W45's unchanged (Decision Log 3); on 77 cells it is a
larger fraction than on 116, which the parent accepts and states rather than re-tunes.

- **Full close:** every target cell within; every gated group's `A` at most the reference's
  `A + τ`; the budget met; every other adopted row passing or its miss named; the referees within
  at the exposure. Lands.
- **Improvement landing:** each target's aggregate (P both poses pooled, C rest, F inactive) at
  most half of the reference's; every gated group's `A` at most the reference's `A + τ`; the
  budget met; every referee within or an unchanged miss; every other adopted row passing or its
  miss named. Lands as improved, every target cell not within named.
- **An exposed cell past the budget or past `3B`** is a holdout miss: recorded, never re-read,
  ruled by the parent as shipped-and-named or not shipped. A gate that reads NEITHER goes to the
  user as W45's did (Decision Log 8 there), with the aggregates and the named cells.
- **Otherwise** the wave closes at the finding.
