## Purpose

**What the wave is for.** The least possible gap to Apple's material on the dark scheme at the
clearer glass, which the grounding read found to be the widest WebGPU gap the bed carries: 124 of
144 non-holdout T1 cells miss on dark 0.25 (S 85.85 against 26.65 for 1x light 0.25 and 6.11 for
the per-span tap width W45 deferred), the same shape as the shipped-default dark 0.5 (106 of 128;
230 of 272 over both positions). The shape is one thing seen three ways:

1. **The photo body is flat.** P reads ×0.25–0.48 of Apple's interior structure in both poses,
   at both scales, on every photo cell (9 of 9 miss per scale; the holdout's three photo cells
   per scale miss too). The tracker has named its cause since W43 G3: the dark
   `optics.regular.tintAlpha` 0.9 transmits a tenth of the backdrop, and W43 Decision Log 7 item 3
   held it at its 0.5 value while the dark scatter probes (c01d, c01g) moved checkers and not
   photo.
2. **The rest checkers are under-structured.** C rest reads ×0.54–0.76 (38 of 44 miss per
   scale), the thin cells worst: `checkerboard-64__capsule-button__rest` ×0.25, `checkerboard-32`
   capsule ×0.35, `checkerboard-4` capsule ×0.23–0.33; `hc-text-7__rrect-sm__rest` ×0.13 at 2x.
   On text the eye sees it as a grey slab where Apple shows the bar (tracker, `hc-text-28`).
3. **The receded fine body is over-structured.** F inactive reads ×2.9–3.6
   (`checkerboard-8__rrect-lg__inactive` 0.0237 against 0.0071; `rrect-md` ×2.9–3.3), C inactive
   ×1.1–2.0, the same over-structure W45 narrowed on the light receded document from ×2.1–2.2.

The dark 0.25 documents differ from the dark 0.5 ones in two tone ordinates only
(`backdropToneResponseThick` knots 2–3 active, knot 3 receded), so this is a 0.5-era material at a
position where Apple's own body barely moves below span 96 (W43 G2) and the slider's dark effect
is the MaxLuma cap relaxing at s ≥ 80 (Memo F). What W46 fits is therefore the dark material's
structure as such; the position is where it may move without a ruling on X41.

**The best version of this is three things:**
1. **Declared targets, each with its family and its prediction, and the ladders before the fit.**
   The three targets share leaves (the transmission reaches checkers and photo alike, which is
   why W43's scatter-only probes found no net gain), so the declaration states per target which
   existing leaves are predicted to move it, by how much, from the rows, and G0's ladders read
   each lever on the renders before part 2 is hashed. A lever that moves nothing is struck; a
   target no lever reaches is named with the operator's shape and not fitted (Decision Log 4).
2. **The level held by the shader's own solve, and measured where it is not.** L1 passes on dark
   0.25 with 0.049 of its 0.055 headroom used and a growth bound of 0.005. The tone ordinates are
   not a curve the body is mixed toward at weight `tintAlpha`: they are the composite levels the
   shader solves its neutral to reach at any `tintAlpha` (`wgsl/optics.ts`, the W9 solve). So
   structure opens with `tintAlpha` while the level holds by construction wherever that solve is
   unclamped at full authority, and the wave measures where it is not — a neutral clamped at 0 on
   bright and mid backdrops as `tintAlpha` falls, and the authority fade just above black — with
   L1 and the level rows at every rung (Grounding, the last paragraph; X61).
3. **The same rule, the same referees' pattern, read once.** W45's growth-only landing rule, a
   W46-owned referee manifest of the dark probe cells declared before part 1, the canonical dark
   holdout read once at the new bytes (read 8), publication through W40's publisher superseding
   `d0219cd684bf`, T1 adopted for the dark profiles at G2 with every miss named.

**What the wave does not do.** No native capture. No light change and no 0.5 change (X60, X41).
No change to T1's statistic, bar or arithmetic. No pre-chartered operator. No re-read of any
holdout at the current bytes. No ruling on dark 0.5 (the user's; Decision Log 1).

**The transmission, as the renderer computes it** (`packages/renderer-webgpu/src/wgsl/optics.ts`,
the W9 tone solve and the composite after it). The body is `mix(backdrop, adapted, presentAlpha)`:
`backdrop` is the per-pixel scattered sample, `adapted` one neutral per surface, and the alpha is
`sizedAlpha = tintAlpha + sizeOcclusionGain·sizeK·(1 − tintAlpha)` (the dark documents inherit the
default gain 0.05). The solve shifts that neutral achromatically so that the composite over the
group's linear backdrop mean lands on `R(encodedMean, sizeK)`, the response the ordinates define
(`backdropToneResponseThin` at span 32, `…Thick` from span 96 where `sizeK` saturates, smoothstep
between; the black ordinates blended in below encoded 0.003 by `backdropToneBlackStrength`).
**The ordinates are composite-level TARGETS, not a curve mixed in at weight `tintAlpha`**: the
solve already accounts for `sizedAlpha`, the collapse (`toneAdapt`), its authority and its
strength, so an analytic re-solve of the ordinates for a new `tintAlpha` would compensate twice.
- *Structure.* The neutral carries none, so the interior structure is the transmitted share
  `1 − sizedAlpha` of the scattered backdrop's: about a tenth at `tintAlpha` 0.9, which is why
  photo reads ×0.25–0.48 and the receded photo ×0.25 in both scales. The prediction for P
  follows from that share alone.
- *Level.* At held ordinates a `tintAlpha` move holds the interior mean exactly where (1) the
  solve has full authority — encoded backdrop mean at or above the dark anchor 0.004, or black
  itself, where the shipped black branch restores it; (2) the shifted neutral is not clamped at
  0, i.e. `R ≥ (1 − sizedAlpha)·L` — there is no darkward alpha solve to absorb a clamp, the
  shader's opacity solve being one-sided and lightward; and (3) the collapse does not own the
  pixel (`toneAdapt < 0.995`; the dark documents' band [0, 0.0001] confines it to a black
  backdrop under the thinnest surfaces). Where it does NOT hold:
  - **clamped.** On the shader's algebra with the documents' ordinates (group means taken as the
    backdrop's: 0.5 linear and encoded on the 0/255 checkers, 0.891 linear on `light-solid`),
    the 0/255 checker clamps below `tintAlpha` ≈ 0.63 active / 0.64 receded where the thick row
    governs (span ≥ 96: the calibration `checkerboard__rrect-md` pair) and ≈ 0.57 / 0.59 at span
    32; `light-solid` below ≈ 0.77 / 0.78 at span ≥ 96 and ≈ 0.48 / 0.50 at span 32. The
    predicted excess on `checkerboard__rrect-md` is +0.015 active / +0.021 receded linear at 0.6
    and +0.062 / +0.068 at 0.5, against L1's growth bound of 0.005; on `light-solid` at span
    ≥ 96 it is +0.058 / +0.068 already at 0.7. `mid-light-solid`, `mid-dark-solid` and
    `dark-solid` do not clamp anywhere on the grid.
  - **at low authority**, between black and the dark anchor: the default fade and the black
    branch's support overlap there, and the authority falls to about 0.22 near encoded 0.0025
    and is 0.644 at W36's lowest packed impulse input 0.00319, so the impulse cells (the
    validation `impulse__capsule-button` pair is in L1) move with `tintAlpha` by the unsolved
    share.

These are predictions from the shader's arithmetic, not measurements; G0's level check replaces
them with the rendered rows, and L1 and the level rows decide. The bed's uniform controls are
