## Purpose

**What the wave is for.** vitrea's goal is the least possible gap to Apple's material. W43 shipped
the clearer glass, the appearance slider's 0.25 position, as a second fixed setting, and its
landing's eye sheets found a gap no gated row had read: at 2x, on the light scheme, the body draws
fine checkers sharper than Apple's. On a deep-body crop of `checkerboard-4__rrect-md__rest`
Apple's 0.25 body is nearly smooth, encoded SD 2.9 codes, and vitrea's shows a crisp checker with
a moiré band through the lens, 9.1; receded, 0.3 against 10.5; the 2x receded photo body shows the
backdrop's diagonal lattice where Apple's is smooth (§5.201 §30). At 0.5 the rest pair read 1.7
against 2.6. The named cause is a fitted leaf, `sizeScatterFloor2x` 1.0 → 0.6 in the light active
document (§5.201 §4), which the fit took to put more structure into the pitch-16 checkers Apple
shows more of at 0.25. The fine-period cells are probe cells, which no cut reads, so the fit's
transfer to fine periods was never refereed, and the user ruled the gap shipped and named.

**What the rows already say** (Grounding, "the structure map"). The published generation carries
the driver's texture statistic on every structured row, and tabulated by span and pose it shows
the gap is three separable findings at 2x, of which the floor's cell is one: thin spans are
UNDER-structured at every pitch in the rest pose; mid and thick spans are over at the fine pitches
only and under at pitch 16 and on the photo; and the receded pose is over at every span and pitch
except the photo. A floor alone cannot be the whole fix, and the charter declares the families
from the map rather than from the eye sheet's two numbers.

**The best version of this is three things, in one wave with no native capture:**

1. **A texture referee that cannot be skipped again.** T1, read over every structured scene at
   0.25 in both poses and at both scales, stated against Apple's own texture with a bar from the
   seven-run archive, adopted in `adopted-thresholds.test.ts` for the light 0.25 profiles and read
   for the others, whatever lands (X51). The fine-pitch probe cells move into a gated role.
2. **The refit, declared first, from the map.** Three moves on 2x-anchored leaves, each with a
   grid, a unit and a prediction hashed before its first fit render; a mechanism-check protocol
   hashed before the ladders that may strike a leaf or fix an inert setting and nothing else.
3. **A light 0.25 generation that is better by measurement and no worse anywhere, or nothing.**
   The 1x rows and the dark rows do not move by a byte; every existing cut is read against the
   published c05 render as its reference; the holdout and the referees are read once at the
   frozen bytes, after the runtime ships them and the owner test's adapters pass on the stage;
   the publication supersedes W43's light generation through the W40 publisher.

**Who it is for.** The same developers W43 served: pages drawing the clearer glass over imagery,
maps and media at Retina scale, where fine texture in the backdrop is the common case and the
moiré is visible at a glance.

**What the wave does not do.** It captures nothing natively (the 0.25 bed, probe cells included,
is in `fixtures/` and `~/vitrea-w43/archive-copy-g1a`). It does not move the 1x light rows, the
dark documents, the 0.5 generation (X41), the macOS 26.5 freeze or the runtime default. It does
not add an operator or a leaf to the material (X44). It does not read W42's holdout H. It does not
refit the 1x texture, which T1 reads at 1x and the Deferred list carries with that reading.

## Design (advisory unless marked)

### What one floor can and cannot do: an estimate, replaced by the ladder (advisory)

For an isotropic Gaussian of width σ and a checkerboard of cell width c, both in device px, the
lowest two-dimensional mode is attenuated by exp(−π²σ²/c²), to first order; text and the photo
are broadband and do not reduce to one mode. The material's tap is a mip chain, not a Gaussian
(`blurSigma`'s doc), so the numbers below are an estimate of the shape, and clause 3's rendered
transfer per pitch replaces them before any family is fitted. At 2x the bed's cell widths are 8,
16, 32, 64 and 128 device px (`checkerboard-4`, `-8`, the pitch-16 family, `-32`, `-64`).

| component | σ, device px | c = 8 | c = 16 | c = 32 | c = 64 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sharp (`blurSigma` 1.25 CSS px; the drawn width is the ladder's to read) | 1.4–2.5 | 0.38–0.74 | 0.79–0.93 | 0.94–0.98 | 0.99–1.00 |
| a middle width | 6 | 0.00 | 0.25 | 0.71 | 0.92 |
| heavy, active | 20 | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.02 | 0.38 |

The floor sets only the SHARE of the sharp component. The map says the thick-span body is over at
c = 8 and under at c = 32 at once, and the two components the deep body has are one that passes
both and one that passes neither; a component near the middle row passes c = 32 and erases c = 8,
which is the shape finding (ii) asks for, and the material carries one, inert (`sizeHeavySecond*`).
Under W42's reading of Apple's declared tree (LT: a narrow blur whose width scales with the
declared opacity and a wide blur composited at the slider's weight), the clearer glass is the
narrow term carrying more of the body. **This is the prediction behind move 1; the ladder and the
fit decide it.**

### T1: the texture row (MARKED; Decision Log 2)

- **Statistic.** `interiorStdDev`: the luminance SD in linear light over the native silhouette
  bounded to the declared region, web against native on the same cell. It is the number M2 and
  the tables already read, every published row carries it, and on c05 it reproduces the map.
- **Support and encoding, stated.** The whole native silhouette, rim and lens band included;
  linear light. One code at a cell is the linear step of one encoded sRGB code at the cell's
  native interior mean (about 0.004 at a mid-grey body and 0.006 at a light one).
- **Population.** The light scheme, both scales, both tiers, every scene whose backdrop is
  `checkerboard`, `checkerboard-lc16`, `checkerboard-4`, `-8`, `-32`, `-64`, `hc-text`,
  `hc-text-7`, `hc-text-28`, `impulse` or `photo`, in every set. Four strata (Decision Log 7
  moved `hc-text-7` out of F after G0): **F**, the fine pitches (`checkerboard-4`, `-8`); **T**,
  the fine text (`hc-text-7`), read in two bands; **C**, the coarse ones (the pitch-16 family,
  `-32`, `-64`, `hc-text`, `hc-text-28`, `impulse`); **P**, the photo. The dark scheme is read
  and reported, not gated.
- **The text stratum's two bands (Decision Log 7).** On a T cell T1 under-reads (0.79–0.89 on c05)
  while the fine band reads ×1.9–8.5 over: the broad text bar is under-passed and the glyph
  edges over-passed, and any lever that removes the fine structure takes T1 further under. So a
  T cell is read on **T1-fine** (the σ 4 device px residual, the silhouette eroded 4 CSS px) for
  fidelity and change, and on **T1-low** (the σ 4 low-pass's SD over the same support) for the
  regression budget only; T1 itself is recorded. The full close does not require a T cell
  within; the landing's `away` veto on a T cell reads T1-low's error growth.
- **Bar.** Per cell, from the seven runs (clause 4): `0.5 code + half the largest pairwise
  separation of the run values`, W39's rule on this statistic.
- **Three outputs per cell, each executable and reported separately.** Per cell let `n` be
  native, `c` the c05 reading, `k` the candidate's, `B = max(1 code, 2·bar)` the absolute bound,
  `e(x) = |x − n|` the error, `g = e(k) − e(c)` the error growth and `δ = |k − c|` the
  displacement; the ratio clause is read only where `n ≥ 1 code`.
  1. **Fidelity:** `within` if `e(k) ≤ B`, or if `n ≥ 1 code` and `|k / n − 1| ≤ 0.10`;
     otherwise `miss`, reported with the signed `k − n`.
  2. **Change from c05,** a partition by precedence: `unchanged` if `δ ≤ bar`; else `overshoot`
     if `(k − n)·(c − n) < 0` (the candidate crossed native) and the cell is a `miss`; else
     `toward` if `g < 0`, which includes a crossing that lands `within` and an equal-error
     crossing (`g = 0` with a sign change); else `away` (`g > 0`). `g = 0` without a crossing
     and `δ > bar` cannot occur on one side of `n`. An `unchanged` miss is an unchanged miss,
     never a failure. The examples that fix the precedence are pinned as tests before part 1:
     `n 0.10, c 0.14, k 0.075, B 0.01` is `overshoot`; the same with `B 0.03` is `toward`;
     `n 0.10, c 0.12, k 0.08, B 0.01` is `overshoot`; `c 0.14, k 0.139` at `bar 0.002` is
     `unchanged`.
  3. **Aggregates:** per stratum, scale, tier and pose, the median of
     `|log((k + ε) / (n + ε))|` with `ε` = 1 code, and the counts of each fidelity and change
     state.
- **Gated where, read where.** Adopted for the two light 0.25 profiles on the WebGPU tier (the
  fidelity target), with the CSS tier's outputs recorded beside it as the tier's residual. Read
  on the 1x light profiles (byte-identical to c05 by X48), on the dark 0.25 profiles and on the
  0.5 profiles.
- **Readings beside it, not gated:** **T1-deep**, the sheet's own statistic (encoded SD on the
  deep-body crop) reproducing the anchors; **T1-fine**, the σ 4 device px residual over the
  silhouette eroded 4 CSS px; and whatever band G0 finds isolates the receded photo lattice.

within clause lands it; between candidates at the joint point, the smaller median
|log(web / native)| over F ∪ C ∪ P at 2x WebGPU lands, a tie within the bar going to the fewer

### The landing rule (MARKED; Decision Log 4)

On the final stage, WebGPU tier, 2x light, both poses, over F ∪ T ∪ C ∪ P less the referees,
from T1's three outputs (a T cell's fidelity and change on T1-fine, its `away` on T1-low):
- **Full close:** every F cell `within`; no cell `away` with error growth `g > B`; no cell
  `overshoot`; every other adopted row passing or its miss named; the twelve referees `within`
  at the exposure. Lands.
- **Improvement landing:** the F stratum's aggregate (the median of `|log((k + ε) / (n + ε))|`)
  at most half of c05's; no cell `away` with `g > B`; no cell `overshoot`; every referee
  `within` or an unchanged miss; every other adopted row passing or its miss named. Lands as
  improved, with every F cell not within named in the ledger and the tracker.
- **The regression budget, stated on error growth, not displacement:** a C or P cell that is a
  `miss` on c05 and `unchanged` stays a named miss; one that is `away` with `g ≤ B` is a named
  regression inside the budget; one with `g > B` fails the landing; M2 keeps its own 0.02
  reference-relative tolerance as the regression stop it is, and is not T1's rule.
- **The CSS tier:** its T1 outputs are a residual; its tables must pass or be named; a CSS row
  that moves `away` beyond its table bound is a named tier residual, not a stop, by the standing
  rule.
- **Otherwise** the wave closes at the finding: the measured best is recorded with its numbers,
  no document is sealed, T1 lands in the owner test (X51), and the gap stays named with the shape
  of the next step written.
