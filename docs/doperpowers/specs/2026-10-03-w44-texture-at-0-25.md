## Purpose

**What the wave is for.** vitrea's goal is the least possible gap to Apple's material. W43 shipped
the clearer glass, the appearance slider's 0.25 position, as a second fixed setting, and its
landing's eye sheets found a gap that no gated row had read: at 2x, on the light scheme, the body
draws fine checkers sharper than Apple's. On `checkerboard-4__rrect-md__rest` Apple's 0.25 body is
nearly smooth, texture SD 2.9 codes, and vitrea's shows a crisp checker with a moiré band through
the lens, SD 9.1; receded, 0.3 against 10.5; the 2x receded photo body shows the backdrop's
diagonal lattice where Apple's is smooth (§5.201 §30). At 0.5 the rest pair read 1.7 against 2.6.
The cause is a fitted leaf: `sizeScatterFloor2x` 1.0 → 0.6 in the light active document (§5.201
§4's structure step), which the fit took to put more structure into the pitch-16 checkers Apple
shows more of at 0.25. The fine-period cells are probe cells, which no cut reads, so the fit's
transfer to fine periods was never refereed, and the user ruled the gap shipped and named.

**The best version of this is three things, in one wave with no native capture:**

1. **A texture referee that cannot be skipped again.** A declared texture row, T1, read over every
   structured scene at 0.25 — fine and coarse checkers, text, impulse and the photo — in both
   poses and at both scales, stated against Apple's own texture with a bar from the seven-run
   archive, adopted in `adopted-thresholds.test.ts` for the light 0.25 profiles and read for the
   others. The fine-pitch probe cells move into a gated role. The row is written and rehearsed
   before any leaf moves.
2. **The refit, declared first.** The 2x floor is the deferral's item, and the Design says why one
   floor cannot serve a 4 px checker and a 16 px one at once: the floor trades the SHARE of a
   sharp component whose width passes an 8-device-px pitch at over half, and Apple's 0.25 body
   passes almost none of it while passing a measurable fraction of pitch 32. So three families
   are declared, each in the leaf space the material already has (X44 holds: no operator is
   added), with predictions and a selection rule hashed before the first candidate renders.
3. **A light 0.25 generation that is better by measurement and no worse anywhere, or nothing.**
   The 1x rows and the dark rows do not move by a byte; every existing cut is read against the
   published c05 render as its reference; the holdout is read once at the frozen bytes; the
   publication supersedes W43's light generation through the W40 publisher.

**Who it is for.** The same developers W43 served: pages drawing the clearer glass over imagery,
maps and media at Retina scale, where fine texture in the backdrop is the common case and the
moiré is visible at a glance.

**What the wave does not do.** It captures nothing natively (the 0.25 bed, probe cells included,
is in `fixtures/` and `~/vitrea-w43/archive-copy-g1a`). It does not move the 1x light documents'
rows, the dark documents, the 0.5 generation (X41), the macOS 26.5 freeze or the runtime default.
It does not add an operator or a leaf to the material (X44). It does not read W42's holdout H. It
does not refit the 1x receded photo structure, which T1 reads and the Deferred list carries with
that reading.

## Design (advisory unless marked)

### Why the floor alone is predicted to fail (MARKED; Decision Log 3)

A Gaussian of width σ passes a checker of pitch p at exp(−2π²σ²/p²) of its fundamental, to first
order. At 2x the bed's pitches are 8, 16, 32, 64 and 128 device px (`checkerboard-4`, `-8`, the
pitch-16 family, `-32`, `-64`). The components the material has:

| component | σ, device px | pitch 8 | pitch 16 | pitch 32 | pitch 64 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sharp (`blurSigma` 1.25 CSS px; the drawn width is G0's to read) | 1.4–2.5 | 0.15–0.55 | 0.62–0.86 | 0.89–0.96 | 0.97–0.99 |
| a middle width | 6 | 0.00 | 0.06 | 0.50 | 0.84 |
| heavy, active | 20 | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.15 |

The floor sets only the SHARE of the sharp component. Any share s puts at least 0.15·s, and up to
0.55·s, of a pitch-8 checker's contrast into the deep body, and Apple's 0.25 body at that pitch
reads 2.9 codes over a backdrop whose own SD is tens of codes. At the same time Apple's 0.25 body passes MORE of
pitch 32 than its 0.5 body does, which is what moved the floor off 1.0. One number cannot pass
pitch 32 at a measurable fraction and pitch 8 at almost none through a component that passes both
at over half; what can is a component between the two the material has, which is what the
middle-width row above is. Under W42's reading of Apple's declared tree (LT: a narrow blur whose
width scales with the declared opacity and a wide blur composited at the slider's weight), the
clearer glass is the narrow term carrying more of the body, and vitrea has no term at its width.
**This is a prediction, hashed with the declaration; G0's ladders (clause 3) and G1's fit decide.**

### T1: the texture row (MARKED; Decision Log 2)

- **Statistic.** `interiorStdDev`, the driver's luminance SD over the interior region, web against
  native on the same cell, in codes (× 255). It is the number M2 and the tables already read, so
  no new instrument is introduced and every published row already carries it.
- **Population.** The light scheme, both scales, both tiers, every scene whose backdrop is
  `checkerboard`, `checkerboard-lc16`, `checkerboard-4`, `-8`, `-32`, `-64`, `hc-text`,
  `hc-text-7`, `hc-text-28`, `impulse` or `photo`, in every set. Three strata: **F**, the fine
  pitches (`checkerboard-4`, `-8`, `hc-text-7`); **C**, the coarse ones (the pitch-16 family,
  `-32`, `-64`, `hc-text`, `hc-text-28`, `impulse`); **P**, the photo. The dark scheme is read and
  reported, not gated (no dark leaf moves).
- **Bar.** Per cell, from the seven runs (clause 4): `bar = 0.5 + half the largest pairwise
  separation of the run SDs`, W39's rule on this statistic.
- **Bound.** A cell is **within** if `|SDweb − SDnative| ≤ max(1.0 code, 2·bar)` or
  `|SDweb / SDnative − 1| ≤ 0.10`. Otherwise M2's directional verdict against the reference render
  (c05): **named** if the candidate moved toward Apple and is not beyond Apple by more than the
  bound; **failure** if it moved away or overshot.
- **Gated where, read where.** Adopted for the two light 0.25 profiles on the WebGPU tier (the
  fidelity target), with the CSS tier's reading recorded beside it as the tier's residual, in the
  manner of every material row since W23. Read on the 1x light profiles (byte-identical to c05 by
  X48, so the reading is c05's), on the dark 0.25 profiles and on the 0.5 profiles.
- **The lens question.** The moiré W43 saw runs "through the lens". G0 reads where it sits on c05's
  render against the interior region's geometry; if the region excludes the lens band, T1 gains a
  second statistic over the lens annulus (the contour inward to the lens height, as the layer tree
  declares it) before the hash. Either way the sheets show the whole body.

### The held-out referees (MARKED; Decision Log 2)

The canonical holdout has no fine-pitch cell, so the fit's transfer to fine periods would once
again be unrefereed. Six probe cells per scale are held out BY ARTIFACT: the G1 bed loader refuses
a fit-time bed that carries one, and they are rendered only in the final stage after the freeze
and read with the holdout (clause 7). The declaration names them; the draft's list is
`checkerboard-4__rrect-ml__rest`, `checkerboard-4__capsule-button__rest-tint-orange`,
`checkerboard-8__rrect-lg__inactive`, `checkerboard-8__rrect-sm__rest`,
`hc-text-7__rrect-md__inactive` and `checkerboard-32__rrect-lg__inactive`, chosen to hold out a
thin and a thick span, both poses and a tint at the fine pitches, plus one coarse inactive cell.
The remaining 13 fine scenes per scale are the fit's.

### The families (MARKED; Decision Log 3)

All in the leaf space the material has; no operator is added (X44). Each names its leaves, its
grid and its prediction in the declaration; each is fitted on the fit cells of F, C and P at 2x
light on the WebGPU tier, both poses, in candidate mode in scratch.

- **A — the floor alone.** `sizeScatterFloor2x` ∈ [0.6, 1.0]. The deferral's item. *Prediction:*
  F improves monotonically toward 1.0 and C's pitch-16 cells lose the structure the W43 fit put
  there; no value is within on both.
- **B — the floor at 1 and the heavy width.** `sizeScatterFloor2x` 1.0 and `sizeHeavyTapSigma2x`
  ∈ [6, 20] device px. One per-scale leaf, 1x-inert by construction. *Prediction:* a width near 6
  passes pitch 32 and erases pitch 8, but one width serves the whole deep body, so pitch 64 and
  the photo's broad structure are over-passed; B lands only if C does not and B is within on F.
- **C — the floor at 1 and the second heavy tap.** `sizeScatterFloor2x` 1.0,
  `sizeHeavySecondShare` ∈ (0, 1], `sizeHeavySecondSigma2x` at a middle width, and
  `sizeHeavySecondSigma` (1x) set so the 1x rows do not move: either 0, if `heavyTapPlan`'s
  decline at 0 leaves the mix at `heavy` (G0 proves it by byte identity), or equal to the 1x heavy
  width. *Prediction:* the deep body becomes a mix of a width-6 and a width-20 term, which is LT's
  two scales in vitrea's leaf space, and it is within on F and C together.
- **The receded step.** Apple's receded fine checkers read 0.3. After the active fit, the receded
  document takes its own override of the chosen family's leaves only if the active values miss a
  receded cell by more than the bound; otherwise the receded document inherits.
- **Not a family:** `sizeScatterScaleGain` (one leaf for both scales, so it moves 1x: X48), the
  ramp starts and reach (W13–W15's per-depth law, outside this gap), `blurSigma` (both scales).
  B's heavy width is read as a ladder even if C lands, so the ledger records what one width
  alone can do.

**The selection rule.** Families are read in the order A, B, C. The first family with a point
that passes Decision Log 4's full-close rule lands; if none does, the candidate with the smallest
median |log(SDweb/SDnative)| over F that passes the improvement rule lands; a tie within the bar
goes to the fewer moved leaves. The rule is rehearsed on c05 and on the pre-fit render before the
hash (the memory lesson on improvement rules, W42).

### The landing rule (MARKED; Decision Log 4)

On the final stage, WebGPU tier, 2x light, both poses, over F ∪ C ∪ P less the referees:
- **Full close:** every F cell within; no failure in C or P; every other adopted row passing or
  its miss named. Lands.
- **Improvement landing:** no failure anywhere; the median |log(SDweb/SDnative)| over F at most
  half of c05's; no F cell worse than c05 by more than the bound; every referee within or named.
  Lands as improved, with every F cell not within named in the ledger and the tracker.
- **Otherwise** the wave closes at the finding: the measured best is recorded with its numbers,
  no document is sealed, and the gap stays named with the shape of the next step written.
