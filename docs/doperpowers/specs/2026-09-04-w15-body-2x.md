## Purpose

At 2x the GPU tier's body is wrong in three places at once, each measured
and each held by a decision that named the next wave:

1. **The widths.** The body's two σ are CSS-px quantities — 1.25 and 10 CSS
   px, so 2.5 and 20 device px at 2x — where the reference's kernel is one
   kernel in device pixels, ≈ 1 sharp and 8–10 heavy (§5.55 §1). Right at
   1x, twice too wide at 2x. Read in device pixels the interior's structure
   lands on the reference at every calibration span with no other change
   (§5.58 §2); carried into W13 as candidate A, that reading moved the four
   large 2x rows down 0.006–0.017 and the two small ones up, and the user
   withdrew it as a *landing* (Decision Log 8) — not as a measurement.
2. **The deep value.** Under the ramp sits W11c's span curve: a heavy share
   of 0.4 rising to 1 over spans 32 → 256, which leaves a sharp share of
   0.60 / 0.48 / 0.36 / 0.24 deep inside `rrect-sm` / `-md` / `-ml` / `-lg`
   at every scale. At 2x the reference's deep interior is 0.90–1.00 heavy on
   the three large spans (§5.55 §1) and its sharp amplitude is zero by
   mid-depth (§5.55 §2). The 1x curve fitted at 1x is the wrong deep value
   at 2x by 0.2–0.4 of the share.
3. **The ramp's second scale.** W13's form only adds sharpness above the
   deep value — `max(0, s₀ − sDeep)` — and at 2x the reference's contour
   share (0.14–0.48) lies *below* the 1x curve's deep value on every cell,
   so the ramp's 2x anchors (thin 0.46 / thick 0.17 / far 0.15 / reach 100
   device px) are provisional and inert, verified bit-exact (§5.67 §2).
   Lower the deep value and the same anchors act.

The three are one gap read three ways. The Deferred entry's arithmetic
(floor 0.53, knee 112, ceiling 0.84 — "three scale terms, one of them a
saturation the material has no name for") is what a *uniform* share would
have to be to match the contour readings; the layer tree's reading (§5.55
§2, §5.50 §2) is a deep value near fully heavy with the ramp carrying the
contour. Which is the law is the wave's first measurement, not its premise.
With it come two coherence terms the landing surfaced: the CSS tier's 2x
mix (Decision Log 5 of W13 holds it at the 1x ramp's average because the
CSS tier's own best single σ is *larger* in CSS px at 2x, §5.55 §5) and the
stacked scene's proxy padding, which at 2x derives from that 1x projection
while the GPU tier draws another law (§5.68 §7).

W13's retrospective named the lesson this charter is written under: two of
its acceptance terms were implementation commitments ("the widths in device
pixels", "the band improving at both scales") and the measurement overturned
both. Every end below is one the reference can arbitrate; the widths, the
deep value and the anchors are the form's to decide.

  Mitigation: G0 fits them jointly with the share bounded, reports the
  RMS surface over width rather than one number, and the sweep at G1 takes
  the width as an axis if the surface is flat.

  0.006–0.017). Mitigation: that configuration is the sweep's first point,
  measured beside the deep-value terms; S2 is written against the W13 bed,
  so the widths cannot land without the deep value that makes them right.
- **The ramp's 2x anchors were read under the wrong width and deep value.**
  Mitigation: G0 (b) re-reads them with the width fixed; G1 sweeps them.
- **The CSS tier moves the wrong way** under either projection. Mitigation:
  q1 is decided on G1's prediction for both, with the tier's own measured
  ceiling as the third column; S6.

  by measurement, as W14's was.

- **The accessibility folds at 2x** were never declared for the ramp (W13
  Deferred). Mitigation: the folds apply once on the composed mix; their 2x
  rows are read at the dry run and any move named.
