### Motion

| Law | Corpus file | Grounding quote/passage (short) |
|---|---|---|
| Motion never an end in itself | `rams-ten-principles.md` | "innovative design always develops in tandem with innovative technology, and can never be an end in itself" (principle 1, applied by extension from innovation to motion technique) |
| Motion must not be noticed as clever | `ive-interviews.md` | "A lot of what we are doing is getting design out of the way."; "So many of the products that we're surrounded by want you to be very aware of just how clever the solution was" (named as the failure mode) |

Note: the corpus predates on-screen motion as a design surface (Rams and the Bauhaus texts address physical products; Ive's motion-specific commentary was not captured in this corpus beyond the "getting design out of the way" framing). These three laws are a direct application of already-grounded general principles (function-first, anti-cleverness, restraint) to the motion step, not an invented rule — flagged here for transparency rather than presented as a corpus passage specifically about animation.

## Part 5 — Derivation rules

Added 2026-07-27 after the first eval round surfaced palette convergence: all three persona builds shipped the home hex values verbatim (cream ground, leaf-green accent) because Part 4 was phrased as "copy it in whole" and Part 5 contained no rule licensing per-product hue derivation. The corpus supports derivation, not a fixed palette:

| Rule | Corpus file | Grounding quote/passage (short) |
|---|---|---|
| Accent hue derived per product from its most important function/state | `rams-braun-cases.md` | "FM buttons should be 'blood orange', phono buttons 'honey yellow', with all power buttons 'leaf green'" — a vocabulary of hues assigned by function, not one house color; ET66's lone accent is yellow, "the most frequently used function" |
| Accent sits quietly against the ground; home green is fallback, not requirement | `rams-braun-cases.md` | Rams "very cautious" about color, accepting it only when colors "have to suit the product"; "Colour can dominate a room. And design shouldn't dominate people, it should help people." |
| Ground temperature chosen per product inside the achromatic family | `rams-braun-cases.md`, `ive-apple-cases.md` | SK4: white lacquered metal + natural maple (warm); Ive-era aluminum surfaces (cool silver) — both achromatic, differently tempered |
| Bounded status-set additions, permanent once added | `rams-braun-cases.md` | Braun chart never introduced a color for one product and dropped it on the next |
| Uniform density rescaling without growing the category inventory | `rams-braun-cases.md`, `bauhaus-functionalist-texts.md` | 606's two bay widths; "economical utilization of space, material, time, and money" |

### Mechanical subset

| Ban | Corpus file | Grounding quote/passage (short) |
|---|---|---|
| At most one accent color role; status set is separate and bounded | `rams-braun-cases.md` | Braun functional-color chart pattern (see Color, above) |
| At most two font-family stacks; second is monospace, tabular only | `rams-braun-cases.md`, `ive-apple-cases.md` | Single-family-per-era pattern in both Braun and Apple cases (see Typography, above) |
| No font-weight value outside 400/600 | `rams-braun-cases.md`, `ive-apple-cases.md` | Operationalization of the one-family/weight-carries-hierarchy law; not an independent corpus quote |
| No `backdrop-filter` | `rams-1976-speech.md`, `bauhaus-functionalist-texts.md` | "This is packaging." / "...without romantic gloss and wasteful frivolity" — frosted-blur treatments are exactly this failure mode |
| No SVG filter primitives (`feTurbulence`, `feDisplacementMap`) | `rams-1976-speech.md`, `ive-interviews.md` | Same "no decorative gloss" + "material honesty on screens" laws — a simulated goo/refraction effect has no real material behind it in this persona |
| No `text-shadow` | `rams-1976-speech.md` | "This is packaging." — a decorative effect with no functional or material justification |

**Note on interpreting "at most one accent color role":** the Braun functional-color chart itself shows several colors in simultaneous use on one product (FM orange, phono yellow, power green on the regie 500). This does not contradict the ban — it is the precise evidence for why the ban is phrased as "one *accent*" rather than "one color total": `--accent` in a stances.md-shaped token system names the single primary/interactive role, and the separate, already-established `--success`/`--warning`/`--danger`/`--info` category is exactly where Braun's bounded, one-meaning-per-color multi-color practice lives. No assertion in the Mechanical subset needed adjustment against the corpus; this interpretive note is recorded so a future reader doesn't mistake the regie 500 chart for a counter-example.

## Considered and not shipped

Candidates weighed during distillation and deliberately cut for lack of real grounding, rather than padded in:

- **"Order and proportion" as an independent, citable law.** `rams-1976-speech.md` names this as a section topic in the 1976 speech but explicitly states it was *not* quoted ("the section on order and proportion... is summarized in this one sentence rather than quoted, because it does not bear on these three themes"). Since no actual passage was retrieved, no law was built on it — it survives only as a two-word mention in Part 1's character prose (identity, not a checkable law), never as a Part 3 prescription.
- **The Rams–Ive video conversation as a distinct source of process laws.** `rams-interviews.md` records this was never located as a transcript; nothing was distilled from it beyond what the file substitutes (Ive's written commentary, separately sourced).
- **"My heart belongs to the details" as a grounding quote.** `rams-interviews.md` flags this as commonly-attributed with no primary source located, explicitly weighted below the file's other material. It was not used to ground any Part 3 law — the file's own confidence tiering rules it out as a sole grounding source.
- **The 2018 *Rams* documentary's "If I had to do it over again, I would not want to be a designer" line.** `hustwit-rams-2018.md` is the corpus's own weakest-sourced file and this line is a late-life register (regret about having practiced design at all) that doesn't yield an operational law — it contradicts the practice-affirming register of the rest of the corpus rather than extending it, and turning a designer's late-career regret into an agent-executable prescription would misrepresent what the line actually says. Not cited under any Part 3 law.
- **Otl Aicher's Univers/1972 Munich Olympics work as typography grounding.** `rams-braun-cases.md` explicitly flags this as a *different*, later, separate project from Braun's in-house Akzidenz-Grotesk standard, specifically so the two are not conflated. Excluded from the Typography law for that reason.
- **A literal "one primary action per screen" composition law.** No corpus passage states a numeric cap on primary actions; the closest material (Rams principle 5, unobtrusiveness) supports restraint in general but not a specific count. The Composition block stays with the laws that are actually stated (self-explanatory structure, standardized units, nothing arbitrary) rather than inventing a specific numeric rule the corpus doesn't contain.
- **A specific hex/Pantone value for Braun's "leaf green," "honey yellow," or "blood orange."** The corpus names these colors verbally (`rams-braun-cases.md`) but no source in this corpus gives a printed color value. Part 4's `--accent` hex is therefore an editorial derivation consistent with the named hue and the persona's overall desaturated, sober character, not a value taken from a source — recorded here rather than implied as verbatim.
