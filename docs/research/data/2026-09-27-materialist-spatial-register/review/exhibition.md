## Numbered findings

1. **Check 14 `[legibility]` — fails the check: enabling Reduce Motion during an existing environment dissolve does not stop that motion.**

   **Location:** `apps/demo/src/gallery/exhibition/EnvironmentCanvas.tsx:90–105,120–138`; the contrary record is `apps/demo/src/gallery/exhibition/DESIGN.md:106–110`.

   The effect depends on `reducedMotion`, but its early return compares the selected work with `current.to ?? current.from` before cancelling `stopDissolve`. During an existing transition, the selected work already equals `current.to`. A preference change therefore returns at line 94 and leaves the old subscription advancing the mix. Starting a subsequent transition under Reduce Motion does correctly cut; changing the preference while one is running does not.

   This was reproduced in the live page. After the media query reported Reduce Motion enabled, the measured environment source mean continued changing: 0.5773 at 567 ms, 0.5599 at 610 ms, 0.5412 at 667 ms, and 0.4998 at 762 ms after navigation. The audit's successful reduced-motion policy emulation does not test this in-flight authored animation, so it does not contradict this failure.

   **Fix:** handle an active dissolve's transition to reduced motion before the same-work early return: cancel its subscription, commit the selected destination, and paint/re-measure once. Verify by changing the preference midway through navigation, not only before navigation. Correct the record until that behavior is implemented.

## What holds cleanly

- **Register, environment and inventory — checks 6, 21 and 22.** The register is named and justified before the inventory: the visitor reads a gallery label beside the work, rather than manipulating the artwork through an instrument. Eight credited paintings supply a fixed, full-bleed texture environment. One substantial window and two subordinate ornaments are not a tile field. The window measures 461 × 720 at the audited viewport, with span 461, radius 32 and thickness 8; ornaments have span 52, capsule radius 26 and the same thickness. They are ornaments, not sub-96 windows requiring an exception. Glass covers approximately 27.3% of the viewport, leaving the painting visible around the composition.
- **Grading and honest hints — checks 19 and 21.** The canvas paints grading beneath the actual rounded footprints, leaving the directly visible artwork alone. Each group receives a measured footprint hint, not a constant; repainting and measurement run on every dissolve frame and on scheme/layout changes. Whole-source mean, declared tone/luminance and rendered level are recorded separately. Across the eight audited phases, window means are 0.7189–0.8257 light and 0.2485–0.3306 dark. No captured host is flagged inside the dead band. The locally soft fog and thunder phases are named rather than concealed.
- **Layering, geometry and grouping — checks 2, 8, 9, 11, 16, 24 and 25.** The window is a labelled section; its article, lists, rows and data remain semantic children, not registered content-role hosts. All three hosts state `data-glass-role`. The window corner is the declared concentric anchor. Opaque work images and child fills have derived framing; the data fill separates, the selected fill responds to the scheme, and there is no light-on-light stack. Ornaments occupy their own overlay groups outside the window and stay within its width, at gaps derived from `samplingPaddingFor`. Geometry is invalidated after placement changes. The host remains still while its child scroller clips and masks its own inner edges. No nested glass, same-plane overlap, authored host background, border, shadow or blur was found.
- **Ink and contrast — checks 12 and the contrast/ink clauses of 23.** Semantic primary ink is placed on children; tertiary is confined to rules and the guide track. The record explains why secondary reading text was replaced by primary after measurement. All independently audited rendered lines pass: the first-viewport/scroller minima are 9.21 light and 5.50 dark; the eight-phase minima are 9.63 light and 5.50 dark. The receded audit minimum is 7.75. The record retains its larger per-line sweep instead of substituting a single average. Finding 2 is the separate weight exception.
- **Material, colour and fallback — checks 4, 5, 7, 18, 26, 27 and 28.** Every surface uses untinted regular glass. Clear is not spent, and there is no fictitious clear scrim or alternate depicted material. Identity and colour come from the paintings, typography and window geometry. `?tier=css` mounts the CSS renderer and the audit reports `two-layer`; the separate DPR-2 live read confirms `collapsed`. Both preserve the layout. The forced-colours Canvas panel and authored marks survive after settling. Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast are designed states, not demotion excuses.
- **Motion and pose — check 17 and the remaining clauses of 14.** No idle animation, glass opacity transition or colour-swap press is authored. Environment changes are navigation between works, not decorative drifting. The window follows focus automatically; the supplied receded capture and measurements preserve its content. The two interactive ornament housings use the runtime's press channels and owned compression transform. Finding 1 concerns the distinct page-owned environment dissolve.
- **Honesty and comparison — checks 19 and 20.** The GPU audit reports healthy texture sampling, exact analysis and refraction for all three groups, with zero diagnostics; the CSS pass reports its actual body form. The record explicitly identifies a visionOS window with bottom ornaments as the nearest Apple composition, says no native comparison was made, and states that the material is measured macOS glass with window-scale extrapolation—not calibrated visionOS fidelity.

## r23 source verdict

**`r23 = 1`; `r23Strict = 1`.** There are no menus, transient platters or sheets to arrive incorrectly: Rooms is content exchanged inside the existing window. Both interactive glass hosts are the ornament housings, whose `interactive` binding delegates to the runtime channel writer for press, glow, pointer position and compression. No glass host transitions opacity, background or filter. No open morph platter exists, so the scoped exception for its unpressed plain items is unnecessary; the strict reading produces the same result. Ordinary controls inside the noninteractive reading window are child fills, as the spatial contract requires, not additional interactive glass hosts. The Reduce Motion defect is a check-14 failure, not a failure of these three r23 criteria.

## Judgment

This is the spatial register the skill describes: a museum label forms one coherent reading window within a product-owned painting environment, with two attached ornaments and a restrained, role-bearing interior rather than glass cards. The environment, footprint measurement, independent rendered contrast, runtime-derived spacing and honest fallback record make the choice substantive rather than cosmetic. The broad frosted window and detached ornament gap are visible limitations of composing this macOS material at window scale, and the record names them without claiming visionOS fidelity. No environment, layer or material failure was found; the page is not fully compliant because a live Reduce Motion change is mishandled, with the smaller display-weight mismatch remaining separately recorded.
