Forty-four megapixels. Eleven controls. No menu.

The Sable One is a stills camera machined and assembled in Bristol, forty bodies a week. Every setting it has is a dial your thumb can find without leaving the viewfinder.

£3,290 body only First batch ships 14 November 2026 SX mount

The sensor

A 44.2 megapixel back-illuminated full-frame sensor, read out at 14 bits, with no optical low-pass filter in front of it. We tuned the response for one case: a raw file that still holds highlight detail after you have exposed for the shadows.

Sensor44.2 MP BSI CMOS, 35.9 × 24.0 mm
Pixel pitch5.05 µm
Readout14-bit, 6.4 fps mechanical
Dynamic range15.1 stops at base ISO, measured in house
SensitivityISO 64 to 51,200; extended 32 to 102,400
Filter stackIR cut only. No OLPF, no AA filter
Stabilisation5-axis in body, 7.0 stops CIPA
Shutter60 s to 1/8000 mechanical; 1/16,000 electronic

Three lenses

The SX mount takes three lenses at launch. All three focus by hand, all three take a 62 mm filter, and all three focus closer than their marked minimum. Choose one and it goes on the body on screen.

The body

Two blocks of 6061 aluminium, machined and bead blasted, over a magnesium mid-frame. 486 grams with a battery and a card in it. Two finishes, and the finish is the anodising, not a coat of paint.
