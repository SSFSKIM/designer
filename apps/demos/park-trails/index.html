Conditions

Read 09:40, Thu 10 Sept 2026
Snow
Tuesday's front left 2–4 in of new snow above 6,000 ft, deeper on north aspects. Traction and an ice axe are sensible on Sahale Arm above the moraine and on the last switchbacks below Easy Pass. Everything below 5,500 ft is bare.
Water
The Stehekin River is low and clear. The footlog over Agnes Creek is in place. The Fisher Creek ford at mile 4 is knee-deep and cold before 11:00; cross on the gravel bar 80 yd downstream of the cairn.
Fire
The Desolation Fire, lightning-caused on the east shore of Ross Lake, is at 340 acres and 0% contained. Desolation Peak Trail and the Lightning Creek camps are closed through 30 September. Smoke settles in the Ross Lake basin overnight and lifts by mid-morning.
Roads and access
SR 20 is open over both passes. Cascade River Road is open to the Cascade Pass trailhead at mile 22 and is rough past mile 18. Thornton Creek Road wants high clearance. The Ross Lake water taxi is running; last return 17:30.
Wildlife
Huckleberries are ripe in the Cascade Pass meadows and bears are feeding in them through the day. Approved food storage is required above 4,000 ft everywhere in the park complex.

The wilderness permit

Four steps
  1. Any night out

    Decide whether you need one

    Every overnight stay in the wilderness of the park complex needs a permit, including a bivouac on a climb. Day hikes do not, whatever their length.

  2. 1–15 March

    Early access lottery

    Apply on recreation.gov for a booking window in late March. Results are drawn on 25 March and windows open in draw order. The application is $6 and is not refunded.

  3. 15 May · 07:00 PT

    Remaining permits go on sale

    Everything the lottery did not take opens to anyone, and stays open until 24 hours before the first night of the trip.

  4. From 08:00, the day before

    Walk-up, in person

    Forty per cent of sites in every zone are held back for walk-ups. Collect them at the Wilderness Information Center in Marblemount, or at Chelan or Stehekin for the south unit. Bring the party's names and a second route in mind.
