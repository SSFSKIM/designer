Fast Ice

The Marram Trio

Sofienberg kirke, Oslo · 3 to 5 September 2025
Released 6 February 2026 · 9 tracks, 46 min

    Three September afternoons in a church on Sofienbergparken, with the doors open to the street. The trio played every piece twice and kept the second take almost every time.

    Eiri Halvorsen, piano · Nadia Röst, double bass · Tomas Lie, drums and bowed cymbal.
    Recorded and mixed by Ingrid Vang. Mastered at Strandgata, January 2026.

    Sleeve photograph by Abby Santurbane on Unsplash. Slow Thaw by Aaron Burden, Groyne Field by Gabriel McCallin, both on Unsplash.
