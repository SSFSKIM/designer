W21 G0 table 1 — the response surface's anchors, read from the dark probe

The law is `R(encodedMean, thickness)` (material.ts backdropToneResponse): three
anchors in the backdrop's ENCODED-space mean, a thin row and a thick row, and a
smoothstep between the rows in `sizeThickness = smoothstep(32, 96, span)`. The thin
row is rrect-sm (span 32, thickness 0); the thick row is the rrect-md and rrect-lg
cells pooled (spans 96 and 160, thickness 1 for both because the size law saturates
at 96). rrect-ml belongs to the thick row by thickness but W9's grid puts no rrect-ml
over a solid, so it cannot join the pool and appears below as an interpolation check
instead. Every level is a MEASUREMENT of the dark reference under the declared box
eroded 6 CSS px. Nothing is fitted.

Two sigmas per level, because they answer different questions. `sigma` runs over
every attested run including a state flip; `sigmaMaj` runs only over the runs holding
the published byte state and is the noise floor of the quoted number. `states` gives
the byte-state shares. A cell where the two sigmas differ by orders of magnitude is
bistable and the anchor rests on its majority draw — say so wherever it is quoted.
