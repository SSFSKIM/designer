The alpha's evidence, and the lerp-versus-multiply discriminator.

`bgSd` is the backdrop's own sd under the declared box — the structure on offer.
`pass` is `sd / bgSd`, the fraction of it the material let through, which is the
quantity a lerp's alpha sets directly. `sd/body` is the passed structure relative to
the level. Under a LERP toward a constant tint the passed structure is a function of
the alpha alone, so `pass` is flat while `sd/body` tracks 1/body; under a MULTIPLY
the passed structure scales with the level, so `sd/body` is flat while `pass` tracks
the body. Read the two columns against each other down a column of constant body and
across rows whose body differs.
