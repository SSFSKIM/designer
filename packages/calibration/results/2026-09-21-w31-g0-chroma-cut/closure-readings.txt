== N2 — ratio (iii) carries a level confound, and correcting it reverses §3's light reading ==

  The blurred reference is the BACKDROP blurred, so it sits at the backdrop's
  level while the side sits at its own. OKLab chroma scales as Y^(1/3) under a
  luma-only change, so the comparison has to divide by (Y_side/Y_backdrop)^(1/3)
  before it says anything about how much chroma the body kept.

  light native, n=25:  as recorded 0.7466–1.0032   level-corrected 0.5161–0.7683
  dark  native, n=10:  as recorded 1.2046–1.7978   level-corrected 1.1749–1.9943

  So §3's "the LIGHT native body very nearly IS such a blur" is an artefact of the level:
  corrected, Apple's light body REMOVES 23–48 % of the chroma a matched
  blur would leave. The DARK reading is strengthened rather than reversed.
