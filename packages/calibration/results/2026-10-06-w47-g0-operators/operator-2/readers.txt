Charter Decision Log 3 v1.1, G0 (f)'s BODY verdict and X66. Assembled runtime 8f55ea1ed.

The sampled-body law, at the existing refracted UV:
  body'    = body + sizeFineTapShare * (fine - body)
  backdrop = mix(body', scatterColour, kScatter)

The share is zero on every shipped material. A resolved width of zero also stands down at that
scale, following the second heavy tap: no texture and no mix, not a source-copy sharpening.
The two CSS-pixel widths form one identity-table group behind the share, never three plain drops.
