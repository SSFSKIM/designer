W47 G0 (a), operator 1 — every reader of the material's base alpha (`tintAlpha`, the optics
uniform's `ou.tint.w`) and of the alpha downstream of it, with what it reads after the operator
lands and why. Charter G0 (a), Design "Operator 1" ("Where it is read, and where not"), X65.

The law, at the one site the optics pass computes the size law's alpha:

  alphaBase(px)  = clamp(tintAlpha + tintAlphaFarAtScale(dpr) * farS(span(px)), 0, 1)
  sizedAlpha(px) = alphaBase + sizeOcclusionGain * sizeK(px) * (1 - alphaBase)

GRADED = reads alphaBase or a quantity derived from it; UNGRADED = reads tintAlpha before the
grading. At the shipped leaves (0, 0) the two are the same number on every pixel and surface.
