# W27c G1b — the activation measurement gate, analysis half (2026-09-11)

**Gate:** W27 coverage wave, child W27c, gate G1b (`docs/doperpowers/specs/2026-09-10-w27-coverage-wave.md`,
Decision Log 12); contracts X1, X3, X7, X8; claims §5.134. Consumes claims §5.128 and §5.130.

**This gate fits nothing, captures nothing and adopts nothing.** It reads committed evidence, it
classifies, and it declares — the experiment, the bed and the bound — before any of them runs. The
30-cell holdout of claims §5.130 is spent; its recorded per-cell numbers are read here, which is
reading the record, and no value anywhere in this gate is selected by its distance to any of them.

Machine tables beside this file, all regenerated by `read.py` from committed inputs whose SHA-256s
they carry: `coverage.json`, `decomposition.json`, `classification.json`. The three declarations —
`experiment.json`, `checking-bed.json`, `bound.json` — are written, not computed.

## 1. What the holdout membership actually is

The inactive scene set is the active split mirrored through `sourceScene`. Counting the 37 inactive
scenes by background and by component (`coverage.json`) shows what that inherited:

| family | calibration | validation | holdout |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `mid-dark-solid` | 0 | 0 | **1** |
| `hc-text` | 0 | 0 | **3** |
| `rrect-lg` (span 160) | 0 | 0 | **3** |
| `glass-over-glass` (the stack) | 0 | 0 | **2** |
| everything else | 33 | 12 | 11 |

Four families are holdout-only, and the fit therefore identified **no** setting of any declared
field on them. Four of the five residuals claims §5.130 §7 names live in exactly three of those
four. That is not a coincidence and it is not overfitting in the usual sense: it is extrapolation
into regions of the declared input axes that the fitting bed does not contain.

The fourth family is the control that keeps this from being a law. `hc-text`, at backdrop encoded
mean 0.740, is equally unrepresented and its three holdout cells read full-canvas ΔE 0.000946,
0.000886 and 0.002736 — among the best on the bed. An unseen family generalises fine when the law's
anchors bracket it. What fails is an unseen family that is **itself** a law's anchor, or that lies
past where the law's anchors stop.

Two of the four are exactly that:

- `backdropToneAnchorX` is `[0.1104, 0.2706, 0.9505]` — the three solid backgrounds' encoded-space
  means. **0.2706 is 69/255, `mid-dark-solid` exactly.** The middle ordinate of the response is not
  a shape parameter fitted through that cell; it *is* that cell's predicted level. The active
  profiles' middle ordinates are direct probe-bed measurements (W9 in light; W21 G0 in dark, reading
  `mid-dark-solid encoded mean 0.2706 thin 0.0284 thick 0.0238`). The recovered inactive bed has 37
  scenes and no probe grid, and its one mid-dark cell is holdout, so the inactive middle ordinate was
  fitted as a shape knob for cells at other abscissas.
- `sizeScatterRampStartFar1x` and `…Far2x` exist for `rrect-lg`, and the active material's own doc
  comment says so in terms: *"The cell the constant exists for is holdout."* The inactive patch does
  not set `Far1x` at all — it inherits the active 0.2 — and sets `Far2x` to 0.04 "by continuation"
  with the thick anchor.

## 3. The five residuals, classified

Full evidence and per-cell numbers in `classification.json`. One line each, then what decides it.

| | residual | classification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (a) | the mid-dark-solid capsule the three-anchor response misidentifies | **bed** — evidence absent, not fixture suspect |
| (b) | the light checker `rrect-lg`'s far-span scatter | **model-form**, with an unfitted-field component |
| (c) | the dark photo `rrect-lg`'s colour/spatial miss | **model-form** |
| (d) | the light photo stacks | **metrology** — the measured plane is not the endpoint's plane |
| (e) | full-strength neutral tint removing the photo's background chroma | **model-form** |

### (a) — bed

The field is the right field and it holds the wrong number. A capsule at span 44 takes 0.024 of the
thick row, so its output is the thin ordinate to within 0.0006: the fitted dark `thin[1]` is 0.089
and the web body reads 0.08866; the fitted light `thin[1]` is 0.4 and the web body reads 0.40198.
Native wants 0.04092 (dark) and 0.45079 (light). Setting the ordinate expresses it, so this is not
model-form.

Nor is the fixture suspect. `mid-dark-solid` is a uniform 69/255 patch, and the 1x and 2x native
inactive readings — two independently captured files — agree to 1e-4 in both schemes (0.04105 /
0.04101 dark, 0.44673 / 0.44813 light, footprint mask, G0 census). The uniform-solid family's
calibration siblings are essentially exact: `dark-solid__capsule-button__inactive` reads body ΔE
0.00000, `light-solid__capsule-button__inactive` 0.00271, `light-solid__rrect-md__inactive` 0.00000.
A uniform patch has no spatial structure for framing or erosion to bite on and the error is 25
display codes, so metrology is not the limit either.

What is missing is the **evidence the active profile had and the inactive endpoint did not**: a
probe grid at the middle anchor. No new field and no new law would close it.

### (b) — model-form, with an unfitted-field component

Part of this is (a)'s cause again: the far anchors were never fitted because their cell is holdout.
What makes it model-form is the **active** bed's own answer. Where `far1x = 0.20` and `far2x = 0.21`
*were* fitted, the canonical matrix still reads `checkerboard__rrect-lg__rest` interior SD 1.206×
native at 1x and 1.325× at 2x, against 0.87–1.07× at every span the bed anchors. A fitted far anchor
buys authority and does not close span 160 even in the pose it was fitted for.

The inactive demand is larger. Native eroded-body SD falls 0.06387 (span 96) → 0.01742 (span 160) at
1x light, a 3.7× step; web falls 0.06073 → 0.04695, a 1.29× step. In the G0 census the reference's
inactive/active SD ratio is 0.76–0.79 at spans 32 and 44, 0.614 at 96 and **0.457 at 160**: the
recede's own scatter is a function of span that three fixed span anchors are not shaped to carry.

Not bed — the same family's calibration siblings on the same recovered fixtures read web/native SD
1.07, 1.00 and 0.95 at spans 32, 44 and 96. Not metrology — 0.0295 of linear Y in SD is two orders
above the 8-bit floor, and the cell's mean is within 0.0026 of native.

*Candidate model form, named and not fitted:* a span-continued scatter law rather than three fixed
span anchors — the heavy share, or the heavy tap's width, rising monotonically past `sizeSpanMax`,
where today `sizeThickness` saturates at span 96 and every span above it is one far anchor.

### (c) and (e) — model-form, one cause

These two are decided against "bed" by their own calibration siblings, and the span trend runs
straight through all three sets.

For (c): the dark `photo__rrect-md__inactive` is a **calibration** cell and reads body ΔE 0.06018 at
1x and 0.05837 at 2x with mean Y within 0.0018 of native — the same failure at the same magnitude
inside the fitted set. The holdout's `rrect-lg` reads 0.06967 / 0.06759. The holdout did not reveal
this; it re-measured it one span up.

For (e): `photo__capsule-button__inactive-tint-orange` (span 44, **calibration**) 0.03483 →
`photo__rrect-md__inactive-tint-orange` (span 96, **validation**) 0.05689 →
`photo__rrect-lg__inactive-tint-orange` (span 160, holdout) 0.07055. At 2x: 0.03517 / 0.05667 /
0.07026. The holdout is the third point on a line the fitted bed already drew.

The cause is one directional contradiction in the declared fields' shape. From the G0 census, the
reference's inactive material retains **more** of a chromatic backdrop's chroma than its active one,
and the excess grows with span — OKLab C ratio inactive/active on `photo`, untinted:

| span | 1x light | 2x light | 1x dark | 2x dark |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 32 (`rrect-sm`) | 1.01 | 1.02 | — | — |
| 44 (`capsule`) | 1.04 | 1.02 | 0.94 | 0.97 |
| 96 (`rrect-md`) | 1.12 | 1.11 | 1.08 | 1.07 |
| 160 (`rrect-lg`) | **1.29** | **1.28** | **1.24** | **1.23** |

The fitted endpoint reaches the recede's darker, greyer level by making the body layer more opaque
(dark `optics.regular.tintAlpha` 0.46 → 0.89) and its tint seed neutral (`tintChromaScale` 0). Both
of those remove chroma. **No setting of an opacity-and-neutral-paint parameterisation lowers level
while raising chroma transmission.**

The tint half is sharper still. `tintChromaScale = 0` was identified on the **checkerboard** tinted
capsules, where the backdrop is achromatic and the reference's inactive tinted body really does read
OKLab C 0.000662 (§5.128). Over the photo the same reference keeps 0.03539 on the capsule and
0.07027 on `rrect-lg` — as much chroma as the *untinted active* photo `rrect-lg` has (0.07488). So
the facet is not "the author hue is removed"; it is "the author hue is removed **and** the backdrop's
chroma is transmitted", and the second half is the part the declared family cannot express. The
checkerboard could not see the difference, and the checkerboard is where the field was fitted.

*Candidate model form, named and not fitted:* the inactive body as a neutral-density absorber over a
transmitted backdrop — level and chroma with separate coefficients — and the authored paint as an
absorbing filter on that transmission rather than an opaque shaded layer. One term would serve both.

### (d) — metrology

The two stack cells' own recorded `actualGroups` settle this with no new capture. Their overlay is
not a sampled surface: it resolves `configuredSource: dom`, `samplingBackend: css-backdrop`,
`refraction: approximate`, `analysis: none`, and paints the **unsampled** material. In the light
scheme the holdout rows record that material as `tint [1, 1, 1]` at `tintAlpha 0.6649600815626966` —
byte-for-byte the flat white of claims §5.129 §3, and byte-for-byte the **active** value, because the
light inactive patch changes no field that feeds it. The inactive endpoint never reached the second
plane, so these cells cannot be evidence about it.

The rows are also stale. `holdout.json`'s own `sourceSha256` block names
`packages/platform-web/src/optics.ts`, `packages/platform-web/src/root.ts` and
`packages/renderer-webgpu/src/wgsl/optics.ts`; all three differ from `main` today, because W27f G1
(claims §5.131, merged `6ae37c1`) replaced exactly that flat with a profile-at-tone derivation — and
it landed *after* this holdout was captured (§5.130 §6 records integration only through `38d782c`).
The two profile documents and `receded-profile.ts` are unchanged and no golden moved, so the other 28
texture-sourced holdout rows still describe today's runtime. These two do not.

What is left once the overlay is set aside is the photo family's own residual, not a stacking term:
the light photo stack's body underlifts by 0.0329 Y where photo `rrect-md` underlifts by 0.0308 and
photo `rrect-lg` by 0.0267, and its body ΔE 0.05075 sits between `rrect-md`'s 0.04771 and
`rrect-lg`'s 0.05921. The achromatic control bounds the genuinely stack-specific part: the checker
stack reads body ΔE 0.01063 against checker `rrect-md`'s 0.00593, an excess of +0.005.

**§5.130 §7's sentence "the one-plane endpoint does not close stacking" is withdrawn rather than
carried.** The one-plane endpoint was never applied to the second plane.

## 4. The declared experiment

`experiment.json`. Four arms, declared before running, each reporting a **reachable interval** of the
declared fields and selecting nothing. Two are runnable today on committed fixtures; two need the bed.

- **A1, the transfer reachability sweep** (decides (c), (e)). Sweep `optics.regular.tintAlpha`,
  `tintChromaScale` and the shade law over a declared grid on cells the frozen bed already fitted or
  checked; record the (interior level, interior chroma) locus the fields can trace; place the
  committed native fixture's pair on the same plane. **Model-form is confirmed** if the native pair
  lies outside that locus by more than the bed's repeat noise — the fields can produce the level or
  the chroma but not both. **Refuted** if it lies inside on every chromatic cell. Runnable today.
- **A2, the scatter reachability sweep** (decides (b)). Sweep the far, thick and heavy-share anchors
  and the heavy tap's width; record the attainable web SD interval at each span of the
  32/44/96/128/160 ladder. Web-only, because span 160 has no admissible native inactive comparator in
  this gate. The spent holdout's native SD is quoted as the prior expectation and **marked not a
  decision input**. The checking bed's fresh span-160 SD makes the call. Runnable today.
- **A3, the middle-anchor read** (decides (a)). Read the two middle ordinates directly off fresh
  `mid-dark-solid` cells at a thin and a thick span, then re-evaluate every cell whose abscissa lies
  between the anchors. **Bed is confirmed** if the fresh ordinates fix the mid-dark cells and leave
  the intermediate-abscissa cells (checker 0.5, photo 0.44, hc-text 0.74) inside the bound.
  **Model-form instead** if moving the middle knot pushes one of them out — Fritsch–Carlson
  interpolation propagates a knot's ordinate into its neighbours' slopes, and the error that ordinate
  currently absorbs has to go somewhere. Needs the bed.
- **A4, the stack re-read** (decides (d)). Record the overlay's resolved state first; if it still
  resolves `css-backdrop` with `analysis: none`, the cell is a W27f reading and not a W27c one. Needs
  the bed, and is best ordered after W27f G2.

**Stops.** Fail-closed capture integrity, inherited from the corrected G1 instrument (geometry
disagreement, non-empty `problems`, a resolved state differing from the declaration, a fallback
adapter — refused, never written). Any swept configuration that moves a golden, an isolation hash or
either active resolved fingerprint stops the experiment. A reachability interval that brackets native
on every cell refutes that arm's hypothesis and the gate reports the refutation.

## 5. The checking bed

`checking-bed.json`. **38 inactive scene ids plus 4 active ones**, in five groups with two distinct
roles — *supplying* cells that measure the reference for the experiment, and *checking* cells whose
vitrea-against-native comparison decides the bound and has never been read. The checking group is
drawn entirely from the **55 declared scenes that have no inactive counterpart at all** (§5.128 §1),
which is what makes it unspent by construction rather than by assertion.

Every id is an existing declared background × component pair given an `__inactive` counterpart, with
**one new background**: `mid-chroma-solid`, a uniform saturated patch at `photo`'s own luminance.
Its justification has the same shape as `mid-dark-solid`'s in W7 — every chroma observation in the
bed today comes from `photo`, where chroma, luminance and spatial structure co-vary, and every
uniform backdrop is neutral, so the bed identifies chroma transfer only where it cannot be isolated.
One level-matched, zero-variance chromatic anchor makes A1's reachability test a measurement.

Both scales, both schemes, and the two 1x light accessibility profiles required; the same two at 2x
recommended, because §5.128 §5 records that the accessibility policies are 1x-only "with no 2x
counterpart in this census at all". Dark accessibility is out of scope — those profiles are not
declared in `scenes.json`.

Per-cell attestation means restoring the ten fields the recovered entries lack (`capturedAt`,
`orderIndex`, `hidIdleSeconds`, `settleIterations`, `settleSeconds`, `chromaShift`, `tint`,
`stateFrequencies`, `observedStates`, `frequencySettled`) **and adding one the manifest schema does
not have**: the inactive-presentation attestation, the negation of `Capture.isActivelyPresented`
proven per cell. `presentedActive` being absent is what all 121 recovered entries do and is precisely
what has to stop being acceptable.

### Cost, and the record's own figure

| | 7 runs/scale (probe bar) | 17 runs/scale (freeze bar) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| standard passes, both scales | 3.0 h | 7.2 h |
| the two 1x accessibility passes | 0.44 h | 1.1 h |
| **total machine time** | **3.4 h** | **8.3 h** |
| with 2x accessibility added | 4.2 h | 9.4 h |

At 9.5 s per cell, the harness's own measured cadence (0.25 s layout, 1.75 s dwell, up to seven 1 s
settle comparisons, 6 s interstitial reset), 80 cells per standard pass.

**W27 Decision Log 5's "one to two hours of the user's machine" does not hold at this bed's size.**
No recorded session matches it: W25 G1's two seven-run passes ran 07:00Z → 12:41Z, 5 h 40 min of
machine time at 158 fixtures per run, and the only "about two hours" in the ledger is ten runs at
eleven minutes on a 56-cell bed. The figure is left standing where it was written, per the ledger's
rule about recorded readings, with this measurement beside it.

**And the machine time is the smaller half.** There is no native inactive capture path at all. The
harness refuses inactive scene ids before opening a window, and `Capture.present` activates the
application and key-focuses the window on every path — the refusal says it plainly: *"nothing here
can ask AppKit for the opposite."* No AppKit mechanism for the inverse is recorded anywhere in the
repository. That work, already named in `tech-debt-tracker.md`, gates the whole capture half.

### The two paths

**A fresh 26.5 inactive session** can decide everything this gate asks, because it is the only path
that reads the reference the frozen endpoint was fitted to. It cannot decide anything about macOS 27,
dark accessibility, or activation timing. Its risk is the calendar: 27 ships **2026-09-14**, and a
machine that has updated cannot produce this bed at all.

**The OS 27 bed** needs the *same* native deactivation path, the same attestation and the same run
count — waiting avoids none of the prerequisite — and it cannot check the 26.5 endpoint. The research
on file (`docs/research/2026-09-10-macos-27-release-status.md`) records that 27 changes the material
with no recompile: stronger diffusion of complex backdrops, a darkened edge, brighter speculars, more
uniform refraction, revised window/sidebar/toolbar geometry. A read of the frozen endpoint against 27
pixels confounds the recede with the version change and a miss could not be attributed to either. It
also needs a full active 27 bed beside it, and two of its axes do not fit the profile key grammar (a
user-facing ultraclear-to-tinted slider becomes a fixture axis the key does not carry; Show Borders
decouples from Increase Contrast, which the `a11yMode` enum cannot express). It is the right vehicle
for a decision about retargeting the project's reference — a larger question than W27c, and the
user's.

## 6. The declared bound

`bound.json`. Stated before the read, on the interior metric, per profile, on the WebGPU tier.

**Clause 1 — the ceiling, per cell, full-canvas.** Every checking-bed cell at or below its profile's
**already-adopted active-bed** WebGPU bound: 0.07 / 0.07 / 0.09 / 0.09 / 0.06 / 0.04. Not a new
number and not derived from the inactive bed — the bar the active material has been held to on the
same tier, profile and metric. The spent holdout already clears it at 14–69%, so this is a
non-regression clause and is named as one rather than presented as the test.

**Clause 2 — the discriminating clause, per profile, interior.** The checking set's equal-cell mean
body ΔE at or below that profile's **validation** mean body ΔE from the frozen G1 read, rounded up to
two significant figures:

| profile | validation mean body ΔE | declared bound |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1x light standard | 0.031024 | **0.032** |
| 2x light standard | 0.033094 | **0.034** |
| 1x dark standard | 0.033466 | **0.034** |
| 2x dark standard | 0.040434 | **0.041** |
| 1x light increased contrast | 0.007716 | **0.0078** |
| 1x light reduced transparency | 0.010442 | **0.011** |

Validation is the frozen experiment's own measured performance on cells the fit never selected on —
what its authors would have predicted for unseen cells. It is not the calibration mean (it is
1.29–2.18× larger, which is the gap §5.130 §8 forbids copying away), and it is a number the frozen
bed produced rather than one invented to be passable. It is also discriminating: applied to the 30
spent holdout cells it would **pass** the two light and the two accessibility profiles and **fail**
the two dark ones — exactly the verdict the per-cell evidence supports.

**Clause 3 — the per-cell floor.** No cell above 2× its profile's clause-2 threshold; one that is
gets named, and blocks G3 from adopting an inactive floor for the regime it identifies even if the
profile mean holds. 2× is the smallest multiple admitting every validation cell of the frozen read
with margin (worst 1.78×) that still catches both dark residuals (mid-dark 2.98×, dark photo
`rrect-lg` 2.05×).

**Clause 4 — what is reported regardless.** Both metrics per cell, plus the resolved group state, the
backdrop tone actually read, the geometry read-back, the per-cell attestation including the inactive
one, and eroded-body level, chroma and SD beside the distances. The per-profile comparison is on body
ΔE; the full-canvas ratio to calibration is reported and is **not** a gate quantity, for §2's reason.

**Clause 5 — stops.** Refused before writing (geometry, `problems`, resolved state, fallback adapter,
missing inactive attestation) and recapturable in the same session; **spent** once written and
compared; **suspended** if the re-attestation cells fail to reproduce the recovered fixtures, which
would make Decision Log 5's admissibility the finding; **stopped** if a golden, an isolation hash or
an active fingerprint moves.

**Holding means** clauses 1–3 on every profile the bed covers. If only some profiles hold, G2 stays
blocked by default — shipping the pose in one colour scheme and not the other is a coherent
alternative and it is a product decision for the user, not this gate's.

## 7. What the record turned out not to hold

Four things, recorded beside the readings they qualify rather than replacing them.

1. **"Seven to eight times worse on holdout" is not a like-for-like comparison.** It is real on the
   full-canvas metric and that metric confounds material error with footprint area; the two sets
   differ 3.4–3.7× in footprint by construction. On the interior mask the light standard holdout is
   1.74× and 1.61× calibration — *below* its own validation set's 2.05× and 1.99× — the accessibility
   profiles are at parity, and only the dark pair is genuinely worse at 3.5×, carried by two cells.
   Every one of the 30 spent holdout cells clears the bound the **active** material is held to.
   Decision Log 12's premise therefore supports a **narrower** hold than the one in force: the dark
   endpoint is unproven, no inactive floor is adoptable anywhere, and four named model-form gaps are
   open — but "a pose proven on 23 cells only" is not what the light and accessibility readings say.
   This is a finding; the ruling is the user's.
2. **"The one-plane endpoint does not close stacking" (§5.130 §7) is not supported.** The stack's
   overlay draws the unsampled DOM material — in light, the *active* flat white at α 0.665 — so the
   inactive endpoint never reached the second plane, and three of the runtime sources those rows were
   captured against have changed on `main` since. Two of the 30 holdout rows are stale; the other 28
   are not.
3. **"One to two hours of the user's machine" (Decision Log 5) does not match any recorded session.**
   At this bed's size the freeze bar is 8.3 hours before attempt loss, 11–15 with it; W25 G1's
   comparable two passes took 5 h 40 min.
4. **The 2x dark `photo__capsule-button__inactive` calibration cell is absent from the corrected
   run** — 8 dark-2x calibration cells against 9 at 1x — which is §5.128's minority-active exclusion
   working as recorded, and is noted here only because it is why the two dark calibration means are
   not computed over the same cells.
