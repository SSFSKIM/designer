W42 DUMPS: Apple's declared glass configuration by span, pose, scheme and scale (no pixels)
2026-09-29. Only `dump-layers` was run: no pixels were captured, nothing was rebuilt and nothing was
committed. The repository, fixtures/ and scenes.json were not touched. Raw data and scripts:
~/vitrea-w42/grounding/dumps/. Everything below is the configuration SwiftUI hands the window server.
It is Apple's DECLARED configuration, not proof of what reaches the screen; the pixels remain the
evidence. Units: pt (= CSS px) unless marked "dev" (device px). s is the surface's short side.

0. ANSWERS
- Every surface carries the same body chain. Three terms matter:
  - a narrow blur, inputBlurRadius 5, in every one of 208 surfaces. Its opacity is graded by span in
    both poses, and by SDF depth in the active pose only;
  - a wide one-sided "blur fill" of radius 8. It is Lighten 0.9 in light and Darken 0.9 in dark, with
    Normal 0.5. It does not change with span, pose, scale or backdrop; only the lighten/darken side
    flips with the scheme;
  - a face colour matrix that is affine on ENCODED values. Light adds a white fill at alpha 0.2.
- One size variable drives every span-graded input: t = clamp((s-64)/96, 0, 1). Nothing moves for
  s <= 64. The upper knot is 160, the largest span in the bed, so the clamp at t = 1 is inferred, not
  observed. Seventeen closed-form laws reproduce all 104 surfaces at 2x exactly (laws.py, error below
  1e-3). At 1x, one law is off only because the receded margin is 1 device px.
- The receded blur opacity is NOT flat across spans. It is 0.4 + 0.4t: 0.400 for s <= 64, 0.467 at 80,
  0.533 at 96, 0.667 at 128, 0.675 at 130 and 0.800 at 160. It is flat in SDF depth. In the active
  pose the opacity is 0.8t at the centre and falls linearly to 0.4t at 1 pt inside the edge.
- W29's "Normal 0.546" was the glass slider's position when W29 dumped, not a material constant.
  Normal opacity equals NSGlassTintAmount at five slider positions:
  - W29's slider sweep (d/dump-sweep): 0, 0.25, 0.5 and 1.0;
  - W29's dump-sdk27: 0.546.
  At the bed's attested slider of 0.5 it reads 0.5 everywhere. The light face fill alpha tracks the
  slider too: 0, 0.10, 0.20, 0.2275 and 0.50 at those five positions.
- Nothing in the declared configuration adapts to the backdrop. dark-solid, checkerboard, light-solid
  and photo give identical inputs: 12 component-surface groups x 4 endpoints, zero differing fields.
- Scale: 1x and 2x are identical apart from four quantities that equal one device pixel at each scale
  (§4). The backdrop capture scale (0.5, and 0.25 on rrect-lg) is also the same at both scales.
- Settledness: --settle 16 matches --settle 8 byte-for-byte, on 4 scenes x 4 endpoints at 2x.
- Against native pixels:
  - the declared face affine gives the native black floor to within 0.6 code in all four endpoints;
  - it follows the light grey ramp to within 1 code for inputs up to 88, then runs 1-3 codes high;
  - the light receded white is the MaxLumaSDR 0.94 (239.7 against 240).
  In dark, everything above the floor is compressed. That compression is graded by span through a
  MaxLuma of 0.6 - 0.6t, floored at 0.35, and appears in dark only. Its sign matches the native
  span difference in both poses (§6).

4. 1x AGAINST 2x
All 26 surfaces in all four endpoints are field-for-field identical, with four exceptions. Each one
equals one device pixel at its own scale:
| input                            | 2x          | 1x      | reading                     |
| KeyFillHighlightHeight           | 0.5         | 1       | 1 dev, all endpoints        |
| KeyFillHighlightEffectOffset     | -0.5        | -1      | -1 dev, all endpoints       |
| backdrop marginWidth (receded)   | 0.5         | 1       | 1 dev                       |
| SDF output maximum (receded)     | 1.5         | 2       | 1 pt + 1 dev                |
- The backdrop capture scale is 0.5 at BOTH display scales. At 1x the backdrop is therefore sampled at
  0.5 px/pt, and at 2x at 1 px/pt. On rrect-lg the density is 0.25 px/pt at 1x (one backdrop pixel per
  4 pt) and 0.5 px/pt at 2x.
- The blur radii are equal at both scales and the pixel widths are close to scale-invariant (kernel
  memo §0). That points to radii in POINTS, with the backdrop scale being only a resolution choice.
  If the radii were in backdrop pixels, the 1x blur would be twice as wide in points.
- So nothing in the DECLARED body configuration depends on scale. Where native 1x and 2x pixels differ
  (kernel memo 2a: light-active rrect-md impulse σ 1.31 at 1x against 2.36 at 2x; receded rrect-md
  5.84 against 5.03), the cause is in the realisation: sampling density, or the device-pixel edge
  terms.

7. WHAT THE NUMBERS IMPLY FOR THE NATIVE CAPTURE (kernel memo §8)
a. Spans. Every s <= 64 is ONE declared stratum: active narrow opacity 0, receded 0.4, no bleed blur,
   no ovalization. Capsule 44, rrect-sm 32 and rrect-48/64 differ only in geometry. Spend the span
   budget on t = 1/6, 1/3, 2/3 and 1 (s = 80, 96, 128, 160), plus one t = 0 shape. Nothing above 160
   was dumped, so the clamp at t = 1 is untested.
b. Depth. The active narrow blur's opacity varies within a surface: on rrect-md it falls from .267 at
   the centre to .133 at 1 pt inside the edge. Centre-only reads (impulse, ESF) see the centre value.
   - Family C (single patch) should place patches at several depths (centre, about s/4, 4 pt from
     the edge) on rrect-md and rrect-lg in the active pose.
   - In the receded pose the declared opacity is flat in depth, so the same depth sweep is a free
     falsification control: it must read one width.
c. rrect-lg is decisive. It is the only surface at backdrop scale 0.25. Include rrect-lg next to
   rrect-ml (s 128, scale 0.5) in the patch and impulse families.
   - With the "σ ≈ 10·opacity" pointer and radii in points, both poses predict σ ≈ 8 at the rrect-lg
     centre.
   - If the radii are in backdrop pixels, the prediction is about 16, and every 1x read doubles.
d. The wide term's λ and w are declared: 0.9 lighten/darken and normal 0.5, the same across span, pose,
   scale and backdrop. Treat them as a stated hypothesis that family B referees, not parameters to fit
   per stratum.
   - What remains free: the wide kernel's width and shape (radius 8, in unknown units; the kernel memo
     bounds s_w >= 9 and reads photo at about 15), and the composition order (lighten before or after
     normal).
   - A cheap lever outside the bed: the slider moves only Normal (= slider) and the light fill alpha.
     A pair of dumps and captures at slider 0 and 1 would isolate the normal-weighted wide term.
     Changing it is a user setting and a new bed key, so it is the user's call.
e. Uniform family A:
   - Light can be thinned to a referee of the declared affine plus the 0.94 cap. Keep 150-240 for the
     1-3 code shoulder.
   - Dark needs spans on both sides of each MaxLuma knot (<= 64, 80, 96, >= 104) and the full level
     range, because its compression is undeclared beyond the floor.
f. Backdrop and position switch no declared input.
   - Backdrop effects in pixels come from the filter's own arithmetic (lighten/darken, clamp, MaxLuma),
     not from switched parameters, so families need not be stratified by backdrop to catch switching.
   - Family D's δ offsets move no declared input. Any δ effect is the filter's spatial support, which
     answers the kernel memo's "W group versus local" question in pixels.
g. The 1x pass (the coordinator's open question). No declared parameter differs with scale apart from
   the 1-device-px edge terms (§4). A 1x cell therefore earns its place only where sampling density or
   device-pixel geometry can matter:
   - fine pitches of 8 pt or less;
   - small patches of 4 pt or less;
   - rrect-lg, whose backdrop pixel is 4 pt at 1x;
   - edge and highlight strips.
   The body-level families (uniform levels, the λ/w referees, δ offsets) have no declared reason to be
   repeated at 1x beyond a small referee. My recommendation: keep only those realisation-sensitive
   cells in the 1x pass and drop the body-level ones. Whether that brings the pass below 22 cells
   depends on what the 22 hold; I have not seen the charter's list.
h. Edge pointers for the separate edge line (W35-W39), with one conflict:
   - Receded hides the SDF key/fill highlight layer, yet the filter's own KeyFillHighlight inputs stay
     live: amount .5, spread 120°.
   - The KeyFillHighlight band is 1 device px high, offset 1 device px inward, at both scales. This
     matches W37's reading that the line is a ramp averaged over pixel areas.
   - The ring shadow is active only (opacity .06, offset 8, stroke 4, blur 5). It therefore cannot be
     W39's receded dark contour.
   - In the receded pose the SDF output reaches 1 pt + 1 dev beyond the path, with blur opacity 1 at
     d >= 0. That is where W39 saw its contour (1-2 dev outside the path).
   - Conflict: in light, that band carries the face's lift (floor 0.52), which would lighten, not
     darken. Why the contour is dark there is open.

- The native values in §6 come from the grounding memo's committed-derived CSV (W34/W39 archive
  calibration rows). No image was opened for this memo, and no holdout, recorded or probe pixel was
