ANSWER IN BRIEF
- Space: sRGB-ENCODED, not linear. Checker black cells and impulse sit at F(encoded mean); every
  linear-light family is out by 19-23 codes (checker) and ~4 (impulse) at any width. F above 150
  cannot rescue the linear mean: F(150) = 197 already exceeds every native checker body.
- Local, not group-level: photo rejects a group mean in luma (RMS 5-9) and chroma (14-46).
- Not a canvas-wide blur alone: the W39 gradients are flattened 0.61x, which a Gaussian cannot do;
  a group blend or a footprint-limited blur can.
- NONE of the six listed candidates closes the checker. Apple's fine-detail transfer is ONE-SIDED
  (bright detail only in light, dark only in dark, both poses); every listed family is two-sided
  and stalls at 5-9 codes. A reading (encoded heavy blur + k*max(0, sharp - heavy), a lighten)
  sizes it: worst-cell luma RMS 1.3 (2x) / 3.9 (1x) checker, <= 0.6 impulse, <= 1.5 photo;
  L1 0 absolute misses, 2 growth fails (+0.007). Not a proposed coefficient set.
- Luma and chroma differ: chroma takes the heavy argument only (s ~10-12 CSS, footprint-limited
  better on small glass); luma adds the one-sided sharp term.
- hc-text fits the encoded-mean pointer at its own footprint mean (177.6, not 128) to

WHAT THE CELLS CANNOT TELL APART, AND WHAT WOULD
Already separated: space (checker, impulse); group vs local (photo); canvas blur vs flattening

   canonical cells <1 code apart. Separator: a gradient or step under spans 32/44/96/160; a blend

   by the checker at pitch 16 only (rrect-md dome 5.2 -> 11.4 from 1x to 2x; capsule 15.4 ->
   13.6). Separator: checkers at pitches 4-64, both scales, 2+ spans, read per pixel.
