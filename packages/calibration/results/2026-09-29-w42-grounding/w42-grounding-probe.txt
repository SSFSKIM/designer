W42 GROUNDING MEMO C: the probe series, the W29 dump, and the smallest capture that still identifies
2026-09-29. Offline and read-only on the repository. Scratch and full outputs: ~/vitrea-w42/grounding/probe/
(python3.12). Units are CSS px unless marked "dev" (device px). One code is the resolution.

0. ANSWERS
- Heavy component W: a LOCAL blur, not a group mean. The group mean loses by 5-10x rms on every pitch-64
  cell. W sits in ENCODED space with sigma_w 12-13 (active) and 14-19 (receded), identical at 1x and 2x,
  so it is a CSS-px quantity. In the receded pose W is limited to the footprint, and the layer's bounding
  box fits best: on the W34 receded capsule the footprint blur reads rms 0.35 against 1.5-2.2 for a
  canvas blur. In the active pose, big shapes cannot tell the two apart and the capsule prefers a canvas
  blur. That capsule is all refraction, so the active footprint question stays open.
- Narrow component C: its width is set by span and pose, and at 2x it is flat across pitch (8-64),
  consistent with a Gaussian.
  - Active, spans 32-44: no blur at all. sigma_n is 0.8 at 1x and 0.4 at 2x at every pitch from 4 to 64,
    i.e. 0.8 dev at both scales. That is the resampling floor of a half-resolution backdrop, which
    matches the dump's backdrop scale of 0.5.
  - Active, spans 96 / 128 / 160: sigma_n is 2.0 / 4.0 / 4.5, the same at both scales.
  - Receded: sigma_n is 4-4.5 at spans 32-44 and 5.5-9 at spans 96-160 (noisy).
  - At 1x only, pitches 4 and 8 pass more contrast than any Gaussian allows (aliasing, section 2e).
- One-sided term, active pose: a lighten (light) or darken (dark) at opacity lam 0.88-0.91 followed by a
  normal mix at w 0.48-0.51. The same algebra holds in both schemes and both scales.
  - A hard max (lam = 1) is rejected by +0.6 to +1.2 codes rms.
  - A soft knee adds nothing.
  - The dump's own numbers (0.9 / 0.546) cost +0.3 to +0.7 rms, entirely through w.
- One-sided term, receded pose: the knee is present, but no family I tried closes it.
  - Pitch-16 knee sides are exactly flat at W, which requires lam >= 0.98 locally.
  - Pitch-64 cores want lam 0.6-0.8.
  - A shared-parameter fit across pitches leaves 0.9-1.5 codes per cell.
  - Hard, lighten-at-lam, soft-knee and two-fill variants all fail the same way.
  - The receded one-sided form is the main thing still unidentified.
- Averaging space on non-binary structure: ENCODED. With parameters carried over from the binary
  checkers (no refit), lc16 is predicted within 0-2 codes; the linear-light alternative misses by
  +3.6 to +4.2. hc-text (three row heights) reads 0.6-6 codes encoded against 16-48 linear. The lc16
  verdict depends on T being straight to within about 3 codes on its unmeasured 160-242 segment.
- Shipped defect: vitrea fixes both widths in DEVICE px (section 6), which halves the heavy width at 2x.
  Both tiers also blur and mix in linear light, on a premise the probes contradict.

7. SHIPPED DEFECTS (not missing laws)
- D1. The body widths are fixed in DEVICE px, so the CSS scale is ignored.
  - packages/renderer-webgpu/src/renderer.ts:600-605: bodySigmaCssFor = optics.blurSigma / dpr.
  - renderer.ts:638-644: heavySigmaCssFor = heavyTapSigmaAtScale(..) / dpr.
  - material.ts:4873-4878: heavyTapSigmaAtScale returns device px.
  - Document values: profiles/apple-macos-27.0-1x-light-standard-glass0.5.json:151-152 set
    sizeHeavyTapSigma 14 / 2x 20; ...-receded.json:89 sets 2x 14.
  - Effect: the heavy width is 13.8 CSS at 1x but 6.9 (receded) or 10 (light active) at 2x. The vitrea
    control reads 13-19 at 1x and 6-10 at 2x.
  - Apple on the same cells: 12-13 (active) and 14-19 (receded) at BOTH scales, and narrow md 2.0 at both.
  - The justification at renderer.ts:582-586 cites §5.55-5.58 ("one kernel in device pixels at both
    scales"). The pitch series contradicts it.
- D2. Both tiers blur and mix in linear light, on a premise the evidence contradicts.
  - WebGPU decodes before the pyramid: wgsl/backdrop.ts:86.
  - The CSS tier picks linearRGB, stating "the reference's body ... is linear in luminance"
    (packages/platform-web/src/css-tier.ts:2136-2150).
  - Every full-linear fit on Apple loses: 5-25 codes on checkers, 16-48 on hc-text, 3.6-4.2 on lc16.
  - This settles the heavy and level argument. For the narrow term alone, the space is not separately
    identified.
- Not defects, but missing laws on the same line: pose- and span-graded narrow width (vitrea sharp is
  1.58 dev everywhere); knee; footprint limit; the 0.5-scale resampling floor.
