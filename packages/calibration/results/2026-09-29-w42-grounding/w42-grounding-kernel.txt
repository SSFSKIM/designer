W42 GROUNDING MEMO B — Apple's body blur: kernel, space, pose, and the capture that identifies it
2026-09-29. Read-only on the repository; scratch ~/vitrea-w42/grounding/kernel/. Units: CSS px unless
"dev" (device px). Codes = 8-bit encoded. Canonical cells are single captures: resolution 1 code; the
W39/W34 7-run repeat bar is <= 0.5 code (575/576 W39 glass cells byte-identical), not measured here.

0. ANSWERS
- Apple's body is TWO spatial scales composited ONE-SIDEDLY. A narrow "content" kernel carries the
  visible detail. A wide component (sigma >= 9, photo reads ~15) sets a floor (light) or ceiling (dark)
  that the detail can only rise above (light) or fall below (dark). The whole checkerboard shows it: in
  light the black cells are flat and only the white cells ramp; dark is the mirror. vitrea has no such
  knee.
- The receded body IS blurred more, in its narrow component only: rrect-md 2.0-2.4 -> 5.0-5.8;
  capsule <= 0.8 (device-limited) -> 3.8-4.7. The wide component is present in both poses, and no
  difference between poses is visible on existing cells. The width change, times the dump's face slope,
  predicts the receded checkerboard swing within 1-2 codes on 3 of 4 light cells.
- Space: the wide component averages ENCODED values before the tone. That is the only order within
  ~3 codes in all four endpoints; linear averaging misses by >= 12 codes. The narrow component's
  space is NOT identified: binary backdrops cannot show it, and the photo discriminator failed its
  CSS control.
- Size and scale: the active narrow width is strongly span-graded (thin ~sharp, span 96 ~2.2). The
  receded one grows mildly (4.3 -> 5.4 from span 44 to 96). Widths are close to scale-invariant in
  CSS px (1x vs 2x within ~15%). vitrea's deep width is fixed in DEVICE px (14 dev = 14 CSS at 1x,
  7-10 at 2x), and its sharp component does not widen when the window recedes.

1. EVIDENCE, AND THE READER PROVED ON vitrea FIRST
Read: canonical CAL/VAL natives for the macOS 27 standard profiles; the canonical web tree (pre-W41,
receded 30fbe05986ae); the W41 G1/G2 evidence. Uniform native levels come from committed derived
files: the W39 archive calibration fits and W36 G0 deep-cut.json (W34 archive). No holdout, recorded
or probe native pixel was opened, and no W39 holdout was read.
Ground truth from code (code-map/; a float64 replica of the WGSL reproduces 11 web cores at RMS
0.27-0.38 and max <= 0.81 code): the body centre is
  enc( R + B·((1-k)·Ksharp*b + k·Kdeep*b - Lbar) ), b = LINEAR-light backdrop,
  Ksharp = chain L1 bilinear, sigma_RMS 1.58 dev at both scales,
  Kdeep = chain L4, ~14 dev and platykurtic (2x light active: L4+0.927, ~20 dev; 2x dark active:
  LOD 3.32, 9.7 dev),
  k per span, scale, depth and pose (dark endpoints add -2·(edge density - 0.03)).
CSS tier: feGaussianBlur in linearRGB, sharp 3.80/4.08 dev and heavy 30.4/19.6 dev (1x/2x), share m = k.

Proof table. Reader: impulse = one- or two-Gaussian ⊛ 4x4 box, affine; ESF = ramp-side Gaussian,
affine, 52 edges on rrect-md.

is invisible. That means sigma >~ 7 CSS at 1x and >~ 10 CSS at 2x. At 1x the impulse reader sees
only the narrow component.

3. SPACE
The wide level on the 0/255 checkerboard, against uniform native readings (W39/W34):
F_li(128)=188, F_li(160)=202, F_li(242)=235, F_li(255)=240 (light inactive); dark span 44/96 differ.
| cell (knee level)             | encavg->T | linavg->T   | T->encavg | T->linavg |
| light act rrect-md 2x (195)   | 198       | >= 210      | ~193      | ~204      |
| light act capsule 2x (192)    | 195       | >= 210      | 192.5     | 207       |
| light inact md/caps 2x (187)  | 188       | >= 202      | 186.5     | 196       |
| dark act capsule 2x (136)     | 134       | >= 151      | 108       | 137       |
| dark inact capsule 2x (128.3) | 127       | >= 146      | 100       | 132.5     |
| dark act / inact md (122/114) | 121 / 114 | [121,134] / [114,127]: not decisive at span 96 | | |
Only encavg->T is within ~3 codes everywhere; each alternative fails in at least one scheme by >= 8.
The dump's lighten/normal weights shift a knee level by about -2 (light) or +3 (dark). The ranking
holds.
Narrow component: NOT identified. A linear-light blur composited in linear light over a large plate
is ESF-symmetric: vitrea's WebGPU halves read 1.70/1.75, even though its blur is linear. Symmetry
separates only "linear blur, then F on the encoded value": that predicts black-half/white-half 1.9
at sigma 2 in a synthetic control, and native light-white vs dark-black halves read 2.00/2.15 (2x) and
2.40/2.70 (1x). That excludes this sub-case only if the kernel is the same across schemes, which the
dump states. The native photo prefers enc by 1.0-1.3 rms. That discriminator is unvalidated: correct on
WebGPU (lin by 0.02-0.15), wrong on CSS (lin blur read as enc).

4. STRUCTURE, AND ONE POINTER FROM APPLE'S LAYER TREE
Pointer, not evidence of record: /Users/new/vitrea-w29-g0-scratch/c/dump-sdk27/ is uncommitted W29 G0
scratch. It holds the macOS 27 26A428 glassBackground DLCAFilter at 2x: light and dark active on 4
scenes, light receded on photo rrect-md only.
- inputBlurRadius is 5 in every dump.
- Blur opacity is graded by SDF distance. Active: 0 at spans 32/44, 0.133 at 80 and 0.267 at 96 at
  the centre. Receded: 0.533 flat, and its distances are zeroed.
- BlurFill radius 8 with Lighten 0.9 (light) or Darken 0.9 (dark), and Normal 0.546, in every dump.
- Face colour matrix black/white: 0.40/1.03 light active, 0.40/0.96 light receded, saturation 1.2.
- The receded pose zeroes refraction, bleed and shadow. Backdrop layer scale 0.5.
The blur-opacity ordering (0 / 0.267 / 0.533) matches the measured narrow widths (<= 0.8 / 2.2 /
5.0-5.8), roughly sigma ≈ 10·opacity: arithmetic on 3 points. Lighten/darken is the knee in 2b.
"Opacity" cannot mean a sharp/blur mix: the receded impulse has no sharp core.

5. CANDIDATE FAMILIES
The argument M goes through the endpoint's uniform response T (E3's F for light receded).
| family                                           | params / endpoint | space   | existing cells         |
| F1 single blur: M = G_s*B                        | 1 (+span law)     | enc/lin | rejected: two-sided ESF, no knee |
| F2 linear two-scale: (1-k)G_n*B + k·G_w*B        | 3                 | enc/lin | rejected: same (linear => two-sided) |
| F4 vitrea form: T(group) + b·(K*B - group)       | 2                 | lin     | rejected: no knee; texture swing 2-3.5x native |
| F3 knee: C=G_n*B, W=G_w*B, N=C+λ(max|min(C,W)-C), M=N+w(W-N) | 4 (+span law on s_n) | enc (W) | consistent; s_n read; s_w >= 9 only |
F3 variants to keep open: max/min per channel or on luma; W a group mean versus a local wide blur;
λ and w pose-invariant (the dump) or not. Memo A's blend m·local + (1-m)·group is F2's special case.
On a binary texture it cannot draw the one-sided floor.
On existing cells: s_n is identified (impulse at 2x, ESF when active). s_w has only a lower bound.
λ and w trade with s_w, and 7-code max residuals remain on the knee fits. W as group versus local is
unidentifiable, because every centred periodic pattern has local mean = group mean.

6. vitrea SHIPPED AGAINST APPLE
- WebGPU:
  - Its sharp component is 1.58 dev in every pose: 0.8 CSS at 2x, 1.6 at 1x. Apple's narrow is 2-2.7
    active at span 96 and 3.8-5.8 receded.
  - Its deep width is L4: 14 CSS at 1x but 7-10 CSS at 2x. Apple's wide is >= 9 at both scales.
  - It blurs and mixes in LINEAR light. The tone argument is group-level: the source mean when active,
    the silhouette encoded mean when receded.
  - The receded level is right because Rbar sits at the encoded mean. The texture is wrong because
    the sharp share (1-k) passes 0.26-0.46 at 1x at B = 0.51-0.54.
  - E3 per pixel on this linear narrow blur lands on F(188): the 210-code failure.
- No knee on either tier: vitrea's black- and white-side ESFs are equal.
- CSS could carry: an encoded-space Gaussian through plain blur() (css-tier.ts:2135-2157 per the code
  read); two blur layers with a mask/opacity (already L1/L2); a one-sided layer through mix-blend-mode
  lighten/darken. The last is unmeasured on backdrop-filter layers and needs a Chromium proof.
- WebGPU would need an encoded chain for the argument; the import currently decodes (backdrop.ts:86).

8. THE NATIVE CAPTURE
All scenes use existing JSON kinds (solid, checkerboard(a,b,cell), impulse(bg,fg,size,spacing),
split, shape `position`), so no Swift rebuild is needed. The canvas is 320x200, as canonical. X5 must
be lifted by the user. Run dump-layers for every scene in both poses.
Per scheme x pose x scale (8 combinations). Holdout (H) is declared before fitting and stratified:
| family                                  | calibration cells                                 | H  |
| A uniform (F >150)                      | 0,64,128,150,160,176,192,208,224,240,255 x {capsule, rrect-md} = 22 | 184,232 x 2 = 4 |
| B two-level checkerboards, varying mean | P1 0/255, P2 48/208 x cells 2,4,8,16,32 on rrect-md (10); P3 96/160, P4 16/112, P5 144/240 x cells 4,16 (6); P1,P2 x 2,4,8 on capsule (6); P1,P2 x 16,32 on rrect-lg (4) = 26 | P1,P2 cell 12; P6 32/176 cell 8; P4 cell 8; P2 cell 8 on rrect-80 = 5 |
|   and contrast independently            |                                                   |    |
| C single patch (impulse, spacing 200 => one square at (100,100); shape moved onto it) | S 2,4,8,16,32,64 x {64->192, 192->64, 0->255} on rrect-md (18); S 2-16 x 2 on capsule (8); S 4,16 x 2 on rrect-64 (4) = 30 | S 12, 24 x 2 = 4 |
| D step under the interior (split at x=160; shape offset δ) | {64/192, 0/255} x 2 polarities x δ 0,±12,±32 on rrect-md = 20 | δ +20 = 1 |
| E isoluminant chroma checkerboards      | 2 hue pairs in [40,150] x cells 4,16 = 4           | cell 8 x 2 = 2 |
| F bridges (canonical checker, impulse, photo; rrect-md) | 3                                  | -  |
Per pass: 121 glass cells, 16 held out (13%), 12 of them structured. The canonical structured holdout
stays sealed for any landing.
How the families separate the questions:
- Contrast at fixed encoded mean 128 (P1/P2/P3): linear averaging moves W by +60/+27/+2 input codes.
- Mean at fixed contrast (P4/P5 against P3) moves the knee.
- The patch sweep gives encircled energy to 64 px. A bright patch reads the knee-passed C and a dark
  patch reads W (swapped in dark).
- δ separates local from group.
- Pitches cover the MTF; four spans and both scales give the laws.
Size (W39 model: 9.53 s per capture, sentinels 16.3 s, 13 s per run, 25 s per pass):
- 121 x 8 x 7 runs = 6,776 glass captures;
- no-glass references for ~66 backdrops in run 1 of each pass = 528;
- ~48 sentinels.
That is about 19.7 h of capture. With W39's quarantine rate (~1 run lost per 10 h) it is about 21-22 h
of wall clock, against W39's 18.7 h for 6,360 captures. Recommended: two sittings by pose, receded
first (~10.5 h each). The levers are the 6 s reset and the repeat count, which W39 ruled at 7. That is
the user's call. Dropping E and the capsule rows of B and C gives 101 cells, ~17.5 h: one
W39-length sitting.
What it identifies that memo A cannot:
- the narrow component's width and shape against pitch and span, separately from its share (A has one
  pitch per backdrop);
- s_w itself and its shape, from the patch sweep (A only bounds it);
- the knee algebra (λ, w), and whether W is a group mean or a local wide blur (δ, patch size);
- the averaging space for W on non-binary structure (A's cells are binary), and for C through
  intermediate-level steps;
- F_li on 160-240, both schemes' span-96 response above 128, and the chroma kernel (E);
- and a structured holdout to referee all of these.
Still unidentified: the private filter semantics beyond effective transfer; the rim zone and
distance-to-edge grading (centre-only reads; this needs edge strips); tinted, clear and
glass-over-glass material; accessibility modes; the recede transition in time; spans > 160; and
non-sRGB content.
