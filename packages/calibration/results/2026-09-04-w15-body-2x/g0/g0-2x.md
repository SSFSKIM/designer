# W15 G0 — the 2x body measured as one object: the base width, the deep value, the ramp, and the CSS tier's ceiling (2026-09-04)

**What was run, on what.** W13 G0's windowed instrument (`results/2026-09-03-w13-ramp/g0/
g0-instrument.md` — the two-component body under `main`'s landed lens, one heavy share per
4 CSS px depth window, solved by alternating least squares) on the committed 2x probe bed
(`results/2026-09-03-w12-lens/probe-2x/`, 56 cells, five attested runs, majority-settled),
pitches 16 / 32 / 64 pooled, five spans; beside it W12 G3's pooled single-σ estimator on the
same bed for the CSS tier's ceiling, and vitrea's own committed 2x captures of the W13 bed
for contract X4. Nothing was captured, no constant under `packages/*/src` was changed, and
the one GPU command this spike ran is the renderer's golden suite for (e). Scripts
`w15lib.py`, `g0a_width.py`, `g0b_depth.py`, `g0c_recovery.py`, `g0d_css.py`,
`g0_tables.py`; every number below is in `parts/*.json` and formatted once, into
`g0-tables.md`.

**What changed against W13 G0, in one sentence.** The share is held inside [0, 1] — a mix
cannot have a negative weight — and the width is swept underneath that constraint rather
than above it; that single change moves the 2x base width from "9 to 16 device px, all
defensible" to an interior minimum at **8–11 device px per span**, makes the 2x deep value
readable (**fully heavy on the two largest spans**), and brings the reach on the three large
spans into one length instead of three.

**Contract X4 travels with every table below** (§3): on vitrea's own 2x captures, where the
share is one number per span, the instrument reads k(u) flat to **0.012–0.046** with every
window within **0.027** of its cell's own mean; on a synthetic 2x ramp at the reference's
widths it recovers the start to **0.001** and the reach to **0.2 CSS px** — and it exposes
one read-off bias worth 11 CSS px that this document therefore reports around.

## 1. (a) The base width at 2x, fitted jointly with the deep value

The question W13 G0 left open (`g0-ramp.md` §7): σ 9 device px is the windowed fit's RMS
optimum and forces sharp shares of −0.17 to −0.45 deep inside `rrect-ml` and `-lg`; the
layer tree's radius law says 16, which makes every share physical at a 28% RMS cost; the
deep-interior pitch-axis fit says 11. The charter's reading to test first: **the negative
shares are the deep value's** — a share fitted near zero under a slightly wider heavy kernel
— **rather than the width's**.

σ_heavy swept 8–16 device px at step 1, refined to 0.25 near each minimum, at the pitch-axis
fit's sharp σ of 1 device px; at every width the fit is solved twice, with the share free and
with it bounded to [0, 1]. The full surface is `g0-tables.md` §(a); the summary:

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `rrect-sm` (32) | 8.00 (0.00507) | +0.450 | **8.00** (0.00507) | +0.472 | never negative | — | +0.00% |
| `capsule-button` (44) | 7.50 (0.00841) | +0.421 | **7.50** (0.00841) | +0.439 | never negative | — | +0.00% |

| `rrect-ml` (128) | 8.50 (0.01603) | −0.211 | **9.00** (0.01668) | +0.000 | 13.00 | **+33.9%** | **+4.64%** |
| `rrect-lg` (160) | 10.00 (0.01696) | −0.317 | **11.00** (0.01806) | +0.000 | 15.00 | **+23.9%** | **+7.81%** |

**The charter's reading is confirmed, with a number on it.** There are two ways to make every
share physical. Widening the kernel until the free fit stops asking for a negative share costs
**24% (`rrect-lg`) to 34% (`rrect-ml`)** of RMS and needs σ 13–15 device px. Holding the
share at zero where the fit wants to go below it costs **4.6% and 7.8%** at the width the
pixels prefer, and 4.1% and 6.5% at the bounded surface's own minimum. The deep windows are where
the two differ: bounded, they sit at exactly 0 on both cells over the whole width range up to
11 (`-ml`) and 14 (`-lg`), which is what a fit says when the true share there is zero and it
is not allowed to overshoot.

**The bounded surface has an interior minimum on every span, and it is not 16.** Per span:
8.0 / 7.5 / 8.0 / 9.0 / 11.0 device px on `rrect-sm` / `capsule-button` / `rrect-md` /
`-ml` / `-lg`. σ 16 costs +45% (`rrect-md`), +60% (`-ml`) and +22% (`-lg`) against each
cell's own minimum. **The layer tree's radius law is not what the 2x pixels ask for**, which
is the same verdict W13 G0 §4a reached on the small spans by a different estimator.

**The two estimators that disagreed in W13 now agree.** W13 G0 §4a's deep-interior pitch-axis
fit read σ_heavy 9 / 8 / 8 / 9 / 11 device px on the five spans at 2x; the bounded windowed
fit here reads 8.0 / 7.5 / 8.0 / 9.0 / 11.0 — within one device px on all five and exactly
equal on `rrect-ml` and `rrect-lg`, the two cells the disagreement was about. The
disagreement was the [0, 1] bound, not the estimator.

**One number or five?** Pooling the bounded surface with each cell's RMS normalised by its
own minimum, the optimum is **8.5 device px over the five spans and 9.5 over the three large
ones**, and the surface is *flat*: within 3% of its minimum over 7.5–10.0 (five spans) and
8.0–10.5 (large spans), rising to +69% and +42% at 16. So one number in device pixels is
defensible anywhere in 8–10 and the probe does not choose inside that interval — which is
§5.56 §1's declared 9 comfortably, and is why G1 should carry the width as a sweep axis
rather than a constant if its rows disagree.

**Sensitivity to the assumed sharp σ.** Over sharp σ 0.25 / 0.5 / 1.0 CSS px the free
optimum for σ_heavy moves by at most 1 device px (`rrect-ml` 9 at all three; `rrect-lg` 10 at
all three) and the first physical width by 2–4 (12–14 on `-ml`, 16 on `-lg`). The width
reading does not depend on which of §5.55 §1's sharp readings is taken.

## 6. What the declaration should carry

Findings, with the evidence, not decisions.

- **The 2x base width is 8–11 device px and the layer tree's radius law is refuted at 2x.**
  Every cell's bounded surface has an interior minimum there (8.0 / 7.5 / 8.0 / 9.0 / 11.0
  by span), the pooled optimum is 8.5 over five spans and 9.5 over the three large ones, and
  16 device px costs +22% to +60% per cell. The surface is flat to 3% over 8–10, so the probe
  does not choose inside that interval: **G1 should carry the width as a sweep axis, not as a
  constant**, and §5.56 §1's declared 9 sits inside the flat region.
- **The negative shares W13 G0 recorded were the deep value's, measured.** Removing them by
  the bound costs 4.6–7.8% of RMS at the preferred width; removing them by widening the
  kernel costs 24–34% and needs 13–15 device px. The deep windows go to exactly zero and stay
  there across the whole width range.
- **The 2x deep value is fully heavy on the two largest spans and 0.90–0.95 heavy on
  `rrect-md`** — §5.55 §1's own deep shares, recovered by an estimator that models the ramp.
  **Candidate (A) is what the deep windows say**; candidate (B)'s ceiling of 0.84 is 0.16 of
  share below the measured deep interior on `rrect-ml` and `rrect-lg`, and §5.64 §4's floor /
  knee / ceiling should be read as what a uniform share would have to be, which is what it
  was fitted as.
- **The thin spans cannot be asked about a deep value at all.** Half-spans of 16 and 22 CSS
  px lie entirely inside a reach of ≈ 50, so their 0.44–0.47 is the ramp running, not a
  floor. A declaration that reads them as a deep value will fit the ramp twice.
- **W13's 2x ramp anchors are within 0.03 of the measurement and were inert only because of
  the deep value beneath them.** Thin 0.46 against a measured 0.47 / 0.44; thick 0.17 against
  0.19–0.21; reach 100 device px = 50 CSS px against a measured 49–56. **The wave's three
  gaps really are one gap**: lower the deep value and these anchors act, on the numbers they
  already carry.
- **The 2x reach is a length.** 49 / 53 / 56 CSS px on md / ml / lg — 1.15× of spread in
  absolute depth against 1.5× in relative depth, and the same shape W13 G0 found at 1x
  (108–144 CSS px). One multiplier on the reach carries the scale; the start needs its own
  span grading, which the fourth form already has.
- **The start does not decline with span at 2x the way the far anchor makes it.** At one
  width the three large spans read 0.192 / 0.199 / 0.195 where the form resolves 0.170 /
  0.168 / 0.163 — flat against gently declining, the difference 0.02–0.03 and within this
  instrument's own level uncertainty. **No evidence for the far decline at 2x, and none
  against it**; the anchor should be swept rather than fitted from these windows.
- **The reach read-off carries a +11 CSS px bias where the profile bottoms out inside the
  half-span**, measured on a synthetic with a known reach of 50. Any reach quoted from a
  bounded fit — W13's 2x numbers included — should be the line through the unclamped windows.
- **The CSS tier's 2x σ is predicted by the reference's own mix and widths**: moment-matched,
  2.91 / 2.82 / 3.64 / 4.19 / 5.01 CSS px against the tier's independently measured ceiling
  of 3.00 / 2.50 / 4.00 / 5.00 / 5.00. What the tier draws today is 3.79–7.04, wider than the
  ceiling on every span; projecting the new mix through the tier's existing gain law gives
  5.8–8.8, wider still, because that law's fully-heavy end is the GPU tier's 1x nominal 10
  CSS px. **If the tier's σ is to be moved at 2x, the term that has to move with the mix is
  the gain's end**, and Decision Log 1 q1 has a number for its third column.
- **The instrument's limits are W13's, unchanged**: the level of k is identified only where
  the pooled backdrops differ strongly in the two components' contrast ratio (the reference
  readings pool three pitches; vitrea's canonical bed offers one, and misses the level by
  0.07–0.24 on three spans of four); the lens is fixed by decision, so its 0.6–1.3 px 2x
  mid-depth residual (§5.55 §4) is inside every residual here; the width recovered from a
  real capture of a known law is biased by ±40% (§3), which bounds how sharply 8–11 device px
  can be believed; windows below u = 4 are excluded; the reference's residual RMS
  (0.005–0.022) is two to four times vitrea's on the same geometry.
- **The transmission moves with the assumed width**, and that is the level degeneracy again
  rather than a term: `rrect-sm`'s fitted t runs 0.412 at σ 9 to 0.536 at σ 8 while its share
  moves by 0.02. No transmission reading should be taken from these fits; §5.61 §6's finding
  that the reference's transmission does not ramp is the one that stands.
