§3. One sided: which direction carries the offset, with σ and the spread held
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A three-observation fit of four free parameters is exactly determined and says
  nothing, so the one-sided read holds σ and the spread at the cell's own two-sided
  values and re-fits the OFFSET and the amplitude on one direction at a time. That is
  two free parameters against three observations and it answers the question the
  direction resolution exists for: a shift moves `below` outward and `above` inward,
  so the two offsets disagreeing is the displacement and the two agreeing is a width.

  `no shadow` means every observation in that direction is exactly 1.000000 — the band
  removed no light, on that side, at every distance. Where it appears the offset is
  identified ONE-SIDEDLY, which is clause 1's condition: the fit still separates a
  shift from a widening on `below`'s own three bands, but the CHECK on that separation
  — the opposite side moving the other way — is unavailable.

§4. The conditioning: the profile likelihood of σ against the spread
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Per bed and per span, on the MEDIAN observation vector of that bed and span. The
  spread is HELD at each value of the sweep and σ, the offset and the amplitude are
  re-fitted; the residual is the profile.

  **What counts as separation.** Not the native-pair bar: the bar is the noise on ONE
  band's `a` (6e-6 to 1.3e-4 on these cells) and the residual is an RMS over eight to
  sixteen bands at 5e-4 to 2e-3 — a hundred times the bar — so the model's own misfit
  dominates the capture's noise and a residual difference above the bar says nothing
  about identifiability. The interval reported is the ordinary one-sigma contour of a
  least-squares fit: the spreads whose profile residual is within
  `sqrt(1 + 1/(n − p)) − 1` of the minimum, with `n` the observations and `p` the four
  free parameters. The bar is printed beside it and is not the criterion.
