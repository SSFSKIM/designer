  §12c. What the native side of the inactive pose actually carries
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    `nat flat` counts the inactive rows whose NATIVE render equals its backdrop, to the
    axis's six written decimals, in EVERY admitted band — a receded window that removes
    no light at all from 3 CSS px outward. Where the affine pair is identified on those
    rows it reads `a` = 1.000000 and `c` = 0. `0-3 nat` is the native departure in the
    body's own edge band beside it — which is where the recede's whole exterior lives —
    and `σ nat` is how many of those rows resolve a native falloff σ at all (the axis
    withholds it below three qualifying rings). Read on the WebGPU tier; the native
    side is the same capture on both tiers.
