§2. The answer, stated over what the table shows
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Two readings, and they are different questions.

  **The generation moved the reach enormously, at every span and on every
  backdrop.** That is the larger half of the review's incidental and it is not a
  backdrop effect at all: macOS 27's receded — and active — exterior is simply
  shorter than macOS 26.5's everywhere the bed can see both.

  **Within macOS 27 the reach DOES vary with the backdrop, and outside the thin
  regime as well as inside it.** The spread lines above are the figure; the
  ratio beside each span says whether it is a rounding or a reading:

  The ratio is a fraction of a SMALL number wherever `above` is read — Apple draws

  spread over a reach of two pixels and says less than its size suggests. `below` is

  **What G1 must carry into its verdict**: a fit of one falloff triple against a
  bed pooled over backdrops leaves a PER-BACKDROP residual at every span, not only
  at the thin ones, and the residual is of the same order as the thin regime's own
  keying. Whether that is Apple's material conditioning its shadow on the backdrop
  or the axis's threshold crossing conditioning its estimate on the backdrop's
  contrast is NOT decided here: the extent is a threshold and a darker backdrop
  has less light to remove. Both readings predict the same table, and separating
  them needs the transmission profile rather than the extent, which is a fit and
  this gate fits nothing. G1 reports the residual per backdrop either way.
