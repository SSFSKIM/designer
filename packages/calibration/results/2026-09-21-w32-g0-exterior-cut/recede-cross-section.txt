§1. How far out does the receded capture differ from its backdrop at all?
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The first offset from the body's LAST pixel at which the capture is byte-identical
  to the background across that whole side and stays so, in device px and in CSS px.
  Offset 1 is the first exterior pixel, so `2` means exactly one exterior pixel of
  that side carries anything at all. The corners are left out of each side's line.

§2. What the one differing pixel is: two columns of the bottom edge
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  `offset 0` is the body's last row, `1` the first exterior row. Read on two columns
  of the same edge — one over a light backdrop square and one over a dark one — which
  says what KIND of term the one differing pixel is rather than only how wide it is.
  On macOS 27 it reads 141–156 over a backdrop of 255 and 0–6 over a backdrop of 0:
  DARKER than both the backdrop and the body over the light square, and LIGHTER than
  the backdrop over the dark one. A shadow is a transmission and cannot lift a black
  square, so this is a semi-transparent dark stroke composited over the backdrop —
  a contour term. And it is ONE pixel wide, with the next pixel the backdrop byte for
  byte: a falloff at the σ ≈ 17 px this bed measures on the ACTIVE pose cannot be one
  pixel wide. On macOS 26.5 even that pixel is absent.

§3. What this settles
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The receded exterior is ONE device pixel wide on macOS 27 and ZERO pixels wide on
  macOS 26.5 — on 26.5 the capture equals its backdrop from the very first exterior
  pixel, on all four sides, at both scales, on both components. Neither is a shadow
  at any alpha, so the recede's anchor solve (W32 clause 4) has no falloff to lower
  on either generation, and §5.166 §7's finding is about Apple's material rather
  than about macOS 27's version of it. What the `0-3` band reads on macOS 27 is that
  single stroke pixel averaged over a three-px-wide ring, which is why the departure
  there is small, why it is larger on the beds whose backdrop is brighter, and why
  this wave's Deferred list is right to call it a rim term rather than a shadow one.
