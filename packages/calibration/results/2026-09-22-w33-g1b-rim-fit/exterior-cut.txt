  makes an order statistic over a span comparable at all (W32 clause 1).

  admitted bands only, recovered exactly from each band's `sampleCount`,
  `backdropMeanLinear` and `renderedLevelLinear` — a count-weighted mean of means is
  the mean of the union. W32 clause 3 solves the six anchors on the restricted one
  because `0-3` holds the body's own over-fill and is of the opposite sign; the
  difference between the two solves is what G1 measures at its first round.
