capture_web now obtains a new observation immediately before every candidate/

retains the complete observation and computed verdict. The output directory is
already owned by the active receipt; no evidence is written into the source tree.

A failed fact is retained as refused, then raises. An observer exception retains
its raw exception type/message with observation:null and verdict:null when no
observation was returned; unknown facts are not replaced with fabricated failure
measurements. If a returned observation cannot be evaluated, the raw returned
value is retained and verdict remains null. Failure to persist prevents launch.
Any such failure inside capture_web occurs after begin and spends the exposure
under the inherited receipt semantics. There is no sleep, wait, retry or alternate
receipt. Prior passing facts are never reused for another profile or candidate.

External preflight remains useful to avoid spending the exposure on predictable
conditions, but never substitutes for these per-process checks. In particular,
previous browser processes must have exited and the machine must meet X6 again
before the next profile can launch. A failing check does not authorize waiting
inside the begun receipt and trying again.
