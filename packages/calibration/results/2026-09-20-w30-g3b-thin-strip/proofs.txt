They render at the macOS 26.5 σ of 15.55. The falloff's tanh argument peaks near
2 there — five times under the ±20 clamp and twenty times under f32's `exp`
overflow — so byte-identity is what the clamp being the identity predicts, not a
coincidence. A golden at a thin σ is what would have caught the defect, and none
exists; the new `@gpu` case below is that reading, taken on a real adapter rather
than baked.
