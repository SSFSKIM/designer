The derivation (full text: platform-web/src/optics.ts, `cssTierBodyE3`)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Uniform backdrop, encoded x per channel, surface luma L: E3 = clip(g·x + k), k = F(L) − g·L.
The production composite in the deep body is α·p + (1−α)·[P·S(x) + (1−P)·x]: L2 is the
identity on a uniform backdrop, L3's overlay (α, p) is the fallback floor, P is presence
(L1 opacity; L3's α also carries P). Keeping L3 byte-identical and solving the filter at rest:

    S(x) = G·x + K,   G = g/(1−α),   K = (k − α·p)/(1−α)

  K ≥ 0: contrast(G/b) brightness(b), b = G + 2K   (§7's order; contrast ≤ 1, no early clamp)
  K < 0: brightness(G/c) contrast(c), c = 1 − 2K   (brightness clamps only where the target
         already exceeds 1 − K > 1, so no clamp E3 would not take)

So the route exists for every α < 1, not only §7's k ≥ 0: on the shipped floor (α₃ = 0.267
over a grey near 224) K turns negative for L above about 128 codes, and the second order is
what covers it. The real domain limit is REACH: a composite cannot leave [α·p, α·p + 1 − α].
Solved against the MATERIAL's own overlay at rest (floor pair on the linear form, untinted
rgba on the encoded form, at the alpha rgba() actually writes), so an author layer folds to
(1−s)·E3 + s·layer, E3 before author tint as the renderer orders it. Channel-uniform
functions need a neutral overlay; a chromatic one (collapse onto a dark chromatic backdrop)
is solved at its E3-luma level and reports overlayChromaResidual = max α·|p_c − p̄|.
Per-surface L means the luma slope on a structured backdrop is g (≈0.93–0.96) where E3's is
F′ (≈0.43); and L2's linear-light blur of E3's output is a second structured-only residual.
Both are for the (b) measurement, not argued.

Enable domain: the renderer's (regular variant, nominal policy on every axis), strength 1
only (a fractional gate draws the old material: the filter replaces it, so there is nothing
to mix toward), presence > 0, and "sampled" = the surface's silhouette `encodedLuminance`.
A hint or a stacked tone stands E3 down on CSS where the GPU tier still draws it (a named
cross-tier residual). Tinted surfaces need untintedOptics (root.ts always passes it).
