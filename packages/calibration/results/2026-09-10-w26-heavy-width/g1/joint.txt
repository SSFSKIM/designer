W26 G1 §7 — one kernel fitted jointly across the impulse tile and `checkerboard-64`
====================================================================================================

Per-tile gain and per-tile polynomial; ONE sharp sigma, ONE heavy sigma, ONE share. Each
tile's residual is normalised by its own signal, so the high-contrast checkerboard does not
outvote the impulse tile by loudness. `separate` repeats each tile's own two-component fit
beside it, and is quoted from `checker.txt` D and E rather than recomputed here.

Vitrea's r0 rows are the CONTROL and its kernel is known: at the inert default the deep
sample is the chain's own level 4, half-maximum sigma 13.42 device px, mixed with a body of
1.25. A joint fit that recovers that from the two tiles together says the instrument pair is
consistent and the reference's disagreement is Apple's kernel; one that does not says the
disagreement is the instruments'.
