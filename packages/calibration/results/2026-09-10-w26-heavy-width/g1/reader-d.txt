### The two kernels the 1x raster cannot tell apart

The decisive statement, and it is about the FIXTURE and not about any reader: a
heavy component of 13.42 device px and one of 25.0 device px, each at its own
share, land in the same 8-bit tile.

  after  quantisation: max |d| 0.01154 linear (2.04 codes), identical pixels 96.7 %

### The low-pass that was tried and did not help

Both the image and every model column through one matched Gaussian, scanned over
its width, on the 1x `rrect-lg` contrast. It quiets the staircase and collapses
the sharp component into the heavy one, and no setting reads the drawn width.

half maximum, kurtosis -0.23. Reader A reduces a kernel by HALF MAXIMUM, so 14.33
