optics pass integrates, reduced by half maximum. `read` is W25 G0's reader A on the rung's
own captures, heavy component, DEVICE px. `ratio` is read / predicted.

  The 1x column stops being a reading of the material at about 17 device px. Reader A's
  window on `impulse` is half the dot pitch — 30 CSS px, which is 30 device px at 1x and 60
  at 2x — and its own delta bound is 60 * scale. The DRAWN kernel is the same in device px
  at both scales at the same σ (the same plan, the same level, the same residual, because
  `bodySigmaCssFor` divides by the ratio and the raster's texels per CSS px multiplies it
  back), so the two columns disagreeing IS the instrument: at 2x the reader follows the
  constant monotonically over the whole 10–25 range at ratio 1.11–1.13 up to 16, and at 1x
  it saturates at its bound. This is a limit of reader A on a 64 CSS px dot pitch, not of
  the tap, and it is what clause 1's ladder has to be read through.

BYTE IDENTITY, which is the sharpest statement of what (i) and (iii) can reach. Against the
inert-default rung, over the 18 ladder rows per profile, GPU tier:
  level offset +0.5      1x: 18 identical /  0 differ    2x:  2 identical / 16 differ
  level offset +1.0      1x: 18 identical /  0 differ    2x:  2 identical / 16 differ
  second share 0.25      1x: 18 identical /  0 differ    2x:  2 identical / 16 differ
  second share 0.50      1x: 18 identical /  0 differ    2x:  2 identical / 16 differ
  second share 1.00      1x: 18 identical /  0 differ    2x:  2 identical / 16 differ

  Every 1x row is byte-identical at every value of both constants and every 2x row differs.
  That is the clamp: at dpr 1 `scatterLod` is already `chainMaxLod`, so a positive offset
  lands on the same level and the second tap IS the first tap, and the two mechanisms are
  not approximately inert at 1x — they are inert to the bit.
