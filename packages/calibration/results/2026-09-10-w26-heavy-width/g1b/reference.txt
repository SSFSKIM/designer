### 3b. Four kernel FAMILIES, each fitted to the pixels, judged in quantisation steps

The control's finding is that the free forty-parameter profile reaches a lower residual than
the kernel the material actually draws while getting its shape wrong, and that a scan over ONE
width does not: on the band where the drawn kernel is known the residual falls to a single
minimum at the LOD the material draws. So the families below are fitted to the PIXELS rather
than to a recovered profile, and the question each answers is how much of the file a shape
can explain — not how close it comes to a curve that was itself inferred.

`resid` is the RMS over rows of each row's residual in units of its own 8-bit step. `free` is
the forty-parameter profile on the same rows and is a lower bound no shape can beat; the gap
between a family and `free` is what that family cannot describe.
