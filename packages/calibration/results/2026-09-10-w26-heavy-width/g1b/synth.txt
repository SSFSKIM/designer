W26 G1b — reader E on synthetics: the smoothness weight, and the reader's bias by shape
====================================================================================================

Every row is the fixture's OWN backdrop raster convolved with the named kernel, gained and
offset to the level and p2..p98 amplitude the real capture of that row carries, and put
through the 8-bit sRGB step. So the modulation the reader has to work with is the modulation
the real file carries.

TWO MTF BANDS, and the second one is the load-bearing one. `S` is the RMS relative error over
the brief's band, 1/64..1/8 cycles per device px; `F` is the same over 1/512..1/8, which is
where a kernel tens of device px wide actually carries modulation. Both are taken only at the
frequencies where the KNOWN kernel's own modulation is at least 1 %, since a relative error
against a modulation of 10⁻⁵ is a statement about arithmetic and not about a kernel; the
fraction of each band that leaves is printed beside it as `cov`. On the brief's band alone a
kernel of σ 13 device px has essentially nothing left to be right or wrong about — which is
the first thing this reader has to say about how the wave's earlier widths were compared.
