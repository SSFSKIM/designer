W26 G2c — the interior in rings from the pane's edge: level and transmission
======================================================================================================================
  rings 4 CSS px wide, the first 6 CSS px skipped (the rim and
  its shoulder are not the body). `level` is the ring's mean luminance in 8-bit display
  codes; `trans` is the ring's regression gain onto the BACKDROP's own luma — the fraction
  of the checkerboard's contrast the pane still passes at that depth.

  A GRADIENT is the difference between the first ring and the deepest: `Δlevel` and
  `Δtrans`, edge minus centre. A uniformly milky pane reads about 0 for both.
