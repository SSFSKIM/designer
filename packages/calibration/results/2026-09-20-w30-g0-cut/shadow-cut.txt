§4b. The instrument on a mixed-span composite
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  `metrics/shadow.ts` returns ONE (amplitude, σ) pair per cell, fitted over ring
  means of the whole exterior with the shadow's edge pinned to the DECLARED
  contour — the union of the component's placed shapes. The charter asks what that
  means where the component has more than one span. The bed carries two such
  components and they turn out to be different cases, neither of them a mixture:

    1x light-increased-contrast-coupled photo__toolbar-group__rest              —  (no width; neither family converged) validation
    1x light-reduced-transparency       photo__toolbar-group__rest              —  (no width; neither family converged) validation
    2x light                            checkerboard__toolbar-group__rest       —  (no width; neither family converged) calibration
    2x light                            photo__toolbar-group__rest              —  (no width; neither family converged) validation

  `toolbar-group` is three 44x44 capsules at 12 px spacing, so its members do not
  differ in span at all — and the instrument returns NO width on any of its seven
  rows, on either bed, in either family. The silhouette is fenestrated: the 12 px
  gaps are exterior to the declared region and interior to the shadow field, so the
  near rings average pixels occluded by two neighbours with pixels occluded by one,
  and the monotone profile the blurred-edge model needs does not exist. The cell
  still carries a departure and a strength peak. **The σ law is not read on it.**

  `glass-over-glass` is a 220x130 base with a 120x56 overlay at offset (0, −8).
  Half-extents put the overlay inside x ±60 of ±110 and y −36…+20 of ±65, so it is
  WHOLLY INTERIOR to the base and never reaches the measured exterior. The reading
  is the base's alone and it is a clean span-130 reading, not a compromise between
  130 and 56 — which is why its six cells sit within 13.25…13.74 of the rrect-ml
  span-128 column rather than between the span-56 and span-130 values. **The σ law
  IS read on it, at span 130 — and every one of those six cells is holdout.**

§5b. The fit's own valley: the amplitude beside the σ
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  amplitude × σ is the profile's first moment — the energy the tail carries — and it
  is what survives the trade. If the product is scale-invariant where the two factors
  are not, the thin regime's σ is a position on the valley rather than a width.
