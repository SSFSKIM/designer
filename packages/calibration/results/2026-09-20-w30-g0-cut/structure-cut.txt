§5. The discriminator: same pitch, different contrast
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  `checkerboard` and `checkerboard-lc16` are both a 16 px pitch. A LINEAR kernel —
  which a positive or signed mix of Gaussians is — has a transfer that does not
  depend on the backdrop's contrast, so candidate (i) predicts the SAME W/N ratio
  on the two. A mix keyed on the source's measured variance or edge density —
  candidate (ii) — does not, because the two sources have different statistics at
  the same scale.

  It can only be run on the macOS 26.5 generation: `checkerboard-lc16` is a probe
  fixture and the macOS 27 generation carries no probe row (§1b). `Tw` and `Tn` are
  each side's own transmission, sd(rendered) / sd(backdrop), which is what divides
  the other side out — `Tw`'s ratio is vitrea's contrast dependence alone.
