STEP 1: SYNTHETIC INSTRUMENT, BEFORE ANY NATIVE FIT

instrument/body.py carries the declared colour families. fitting.py reports
least-squares and minimax separately, numerical Jacobian rank/singular values,
convergence and bounds. edge_law.py carries the 44-coefficient signed/even
basis, 8x8 integration, and the two H4 coefficients. Its default synthetic
geometry is explicitly a local straight; a native curved-path caller must
supply the attested field at each quadrature point.

Synthetic recovery uses arbitrary generated colours, not native observations.
synthetic-attempt-1.json: largest body-family LS RSS 6.12362e-12, largest
minimax RSS 1.19815e-26; all optimizers converge. Ranks 7 (H1), 6 (H3
chromatic), 4 (H3prime chromatic), 5 (H2prime, every endpoint), 2 (H4), and
44 (edge). The neutral seven ordinates are separate from chromatic ranks.
H2 has zero fitted parameters. synthetic-extra.json provides the edge minimax
certificate and wrong-family tests; no native number is in these artifacts.

The synthetic instrument exposes two nonidentifiabilities that the declaration
allows to be reported, not regularized: width2 duplicates the first even and
signed shoulder hats, and H4's body-chroma coefficient duplicates the signed
line's body-chroma coefficient if both are free. No coefficient is silently
removed and no rank requirement or declaration is amended.

H2 PORT PROOF WITHOUT A BROWSER

shader-proof.py extracts the shipped tone response, solve, composite, retention
and gamut WGSL unchanged into a compute wrapper with synthetic uniform inputs.
It ran on an Apple M2 Pro Metal IntegratedGPU through wgpu 0.32.0 in the scratch
venv /tmp/w39-g2-wgpu, with no fallback adapter, browser, capture or native pixel.
432 cases: all four endpoints, spans32/44/64/96, retention0/shipped/1, neutral,
chromatic, black and near-black inputs. Maximum Python/shader error is
0.0000592938 codes (shader-proof-attempt-2.json). This proves optical arithmetic,
not texture sampling or browser compositing. The first run caught an integer
array truncating the light neutral's solved values; its 172.832724-code error
is retained in shader-proof-attempt-1.json, corrected by floating-point storage.

Historical local checks remain beside their corrections. test-body-green.txt
actually records a failed synthetic witness: its selected matrix gave equal G/B
weights, so exchanging G/B could not change R. The asymmetric witness corrects
that test in test-body-corrected.txt (4 pass). test-fit-green.txt records a scalar
Jacobian shape bug; test-fit-corrected.txt passes2 after explicit matrix reshape.
The edge tests pass3. No native data informed either correction. An initial
Node import could not resolve extensionless compiled ESM imports; tsx runs the
renderer's source merge in resolve-materials.ts instead, with the correct
(base, patch) signature. resolved-materials.json is generated by that merge.
