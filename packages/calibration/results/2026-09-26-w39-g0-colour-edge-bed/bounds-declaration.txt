BODY STATISTIC AND FORWARD FAMILIES
E/D are IEC sRGB encode/decode, W=(.2126,.7152,.0722). Work in encoded output
codes for closure. Per-run deep statistic: channel median at signed d<=-6 CSS
px with >=4 pixels, then the median of seven run medians. Keep deep spatial
min/max separately, never as repeat noise. Per-channel/bin bar is 0.5 + half
the largest pairwise run-median separation, computed on all seven admitted runs
before plurality. Losing states stay in the archive and are scored separately.
F is piecewise-linear ENCODED neutral input/output through fixed input bytes
40,56,72,88,104,128,150; seven output ordinates per scheme x pose, shared across
scales. Fit these on neutral calibration only; outside their interval use the
end-segment linear continuation and unit-cube clip, named extrapolation at the
saturated bridge anchors. Do not use validation/holdout to extend F.
H1 negative control: out_j=F(in_j); 7 neutral ordinates per scheme x pose,
zero chromatic coefficients. Already excluded by archive; not newly identified.
H2 exact shipped body: existing tone_response on E(group tone luminance), size
law, toneAdapt and black branch, then linear-luma-preserving retention and
f32 renormalisation and gamut_at_luma, before tint and boundary. With sampled
linear b, solved a,n,t, adaptation k, presence m: a'=a+k(1-a),
adapted=[n(1-k)a+t*k]/a'; c0=(1-a'm)b+a'm*adapted; Y=W.c0; Yb=W.b;
c1=(1-r)c0+r*b*(Y/Yb), renormalise by Y/(W.c1), then compress chroma about
Y*1 with largest u in [0,1] keeping Y*1+u*(c1-Y*1) inside the unit cube.
Use the shader's zero/degenerate gates, not division by zero. Black strength
blends target and authority below encoded .003 and rejoins before .00319488975.
The chromaticity coefficient is q_base+(1-q_base)*r, NOT r. Tone changes Y;
only retention preserves its incoming Y. Exact H2 has zero free parameters.
H2': same algorithm; free bodyChromaRetention r in [0,1] and the four ordinates
of backdropToneResponseThin in [0,1], per scheme x pose (5 total). Keep that
endpoint's shipped backdropToneResponseThick row, four backdropToneAnchorX
abscissae and every other leaf fixed. Keep the thin row monotone and retain the
existing Hermite interpolation. Colour cells are span44, whose thick weight is
small (approximately0.1 in W38); holding the thick contribution identifies the
thin row rather than introducing independent, poorly identified thick knobs.
Fit on span44 calibration colour/neutral cells only. Thick transfer on span64
calibration controls and span96 validation controls is TESTED, not fitted, and
reported beside survival: a span44 survivor's thick miss is a thick-row miss,
not a false rejection of its colour law. The split is not changed by this fit
restriction. Shared thin/thick ordinate shifts are declined because they move
thick behaviour without identifying it. No colour survival waives G3 transfer.
H3: z=D(F(in)); out=E(clamp(M*z,0,1)); fixed 3x3 M per scheme x pose,
M*1=1; six independent real coefficients, third entry of each row derived.
Seven neutral ordinates + six chromatic coefficients =13 per scheme x pose.
Gamut is component-wise unit-cube clipping, not an after-the-fact gamut choice.
H3': convert linear RGB to OKLab; tone on L via seven neutral-calibration knots
at the L values of the same seven greys, and fixed real 2x2 A on (a,b), no
translation; inverse OKLab then unit-cube clip then E. Seven tone ordinates +
four chromatic coefficients =11 per scheme x pose; neutral remains neutral.
H4: a surviving body law plus B(body,b,signed n,d,span), zero for d<=-6 CSS px.
Concrete extra boundary form is L_w(-d)*max(0,-ny)^p *
[u*(b_c-Yb)+v*(body_c-Ybody)] in encoded units, 2 coefficients per scheme/pose;
w,p shared with the edge line below. This is a separate candidate, not an
arbitrary per-colour lookup. Independent nonzero boundary evidence AND
out-of-sample edge improvement are necessary; parameter count is not evidence.

EDGE LAW, GEOMETRY AND POPULATIONS
Use attested supplied frameOrigin and sizes, not assumed centred declarations;
image y increases downward. Supplied paths are not raster-phase attestations.
Circular stadium SDF radius=min(width,height)/2; radius22 continuous rrect uses
its attested supplied path (flattening error <=1/1024 CSS px), not circular
corners substituted silently. Each column member has its own path and body.
Opaque coverage is calibrated separately on ordinary-fill paths; no transfer
of opaque registration to glass is presumed, and per-run alignment is not fit
to remove the variance being measured. Report supplied/raster distinction.
Shells are one DEVICE pixel wide from -14*scale through +4*scale (CSS inward
14, exterior4); paired top/bottom have equal depth. Top/bottom/left/right
straights remain separate; arcs use W37 X13 nearest-normal 16 bins of 22.5deg.
Minimum 4 pixels per channel/bin/shell/member; deficient/absent required bins
are explicit UNMEASURED, never silently omitted as a pass. Deep >=4 too.
Even line L_w(t)*abs(ny)^p plus signed line L_w(t)*max(0,-ny)^p,
L_w(t)=max(1-t/w,0) for t>=0 else0. w in {.8,1,1.2,1.4,1.6,1.8,2,2.2,2.4}
CSS px; p in {1,2,3,4,6}; both scales share a law. Each amplitude is
R_c=a+g*Ybody+(c+h*Ybody)*(body_c-Ybody), four coefficients per lobe, 8 total
per scheme/pose. Keep W37's even shoulder hats at t=0,2,6,12, zero at12 and
outside, independent isotropic and abs(ny)^p response per free hat: six hats
with four response coefficients each, 24 further scalars (32 with lines).
The signed shoulder uses the same three hats times max(0,-ny)^p, twelve
further scalars (44 total per scheme/pose). Every coefficient signed within
[-4096,4096] normalized encoded units. Body means the frozen identified body
response, never a free per-cell edge colour. Span multiplier fixed1; the
44/64 calibration ladder tests this law, and 96 is read-only transfer, not
an extra fitted span polynomial. No coefficient changes by position/gradient.
Area-integrate basis over each device pixel with fixed 8x8 midpoint quadrature,
then colour response, encoded clamp. Check16x16 sensitivity without refitting.
Rank deficiency or an unidentifiable coefficient is reported, not regularised
away without a new declaration. Effective signed-normal response is the claim;
these symmetric paths cannot identify Apple's actual lighting coordinate frame.

FIT, SURVIVAL, CLOSURE AND RESOLUTION
Calibration only fit; validation is read-only transfer. Report least-squares
and minimax, worst-channel and all-channel failures, rank and singular values.
Equal mass per channel/bin, equal weight per cell; no pooling buys closure.
Deep residual=abs(predicted-native seven-run median) per cell/channel.
Edge residual=mean(abs(predicted-native RGB)) per channel/bin, absolute before
spatial reduction; signed means diagnostic only. Every repeat state is scored
against frozen predictions beside the median. The bar is never fit to residuals.
SURVIVAL requires every required calibration AND validation channel/bin to meet
max(1 code,bar) before freezing parameters/endpoints/forward outputs by artifact.
Only survivors reach the once-only W39 receipt-bound holdout exposure. Closure
requires the same test on all six held-out colours in ALL EIGHT strata and all
wave holdout geometry pairs. Missing/censored required comparisons=UNMEASURED.
Surviving predictions differing by less than max(3 codes,sum of applicable bars)
at every admitted discriminator are insufficient resolution, not identification
and not permission to widen tolerance. Failure or censoring never re-cuts split.

PHASE PREFLIGHT: ONE ACTUATOR, TWO PREDECLARED BRANCHES
40 light-active captures: 9 geometries x opaque/glass x 2 scales (36), plus
phase-zero opaque/glass repeated at each scale's END (4), measuring temporal
drift separately from actuator effects. Radius22
rrect120x44; left/top fixed. Fractional width/height increments are device
{0,.25,.5,.75}/scale CSS px; position=pinned edge+size/2. Shared zero, three
width, three height, plus integer width121 and height45 controls at phase0.
For EACH axis at BOTH scales: opaque near-edge profile byte-identical across
four phases; far edge >=3 distinct coverage states. Glass near-edge shells
both sides of boundary stay within max(0.5,D_int_near), where D_int_near is the
aligned integer-size control's near-band difference; this bounds whole-pixel
size sensitivity, not an invented repeat bar. The phase-zero end sentinel must
match its opening coverage bytes and glass within0.5; otherwise the scale is
UNMEASURED for drift, not unreachable by actuator and not admitted.
Glass far edge has >=3 distinct states with pairwise differences greater than
max(0.5,D_int_far), the aligned integer control's far-profile difference.
States are counted greedily in requested phase order; a new state must differ
from EVERY retained state beyond this bound. Integer far profiles are aligned
by their whole-device shift (scale device px), retaining material differences.
SHIFT model: one shared profile (step plus piecewise-linear knots spaced every
1.0 DEVICE px over -14 CSS inward to +4 CSS outward), integrated over pixels,
translated by four fitted per-phase edge offsets. AMPLITUDE model: the same
knot count/shared profile at ONE shared edge, with four phase gains multiplying
(profile minus exterior level). Compare equally parameterised profiles.
All criteria hold: RSS_shift<=0.5*RSS_amplitude; fitted edges nondecreasing with
requested phase within1/32 device px; shared-profile design matrix full rank,
condition number reported (not identified if rank deficient). Leave one phase
out: fit shared profile and three edges on the other phases, then fit ONLY the
held-out edge; every knot-sampled coordinate/channel residual must be <=
max(0.5,D_int_far)+0.5*||w_i||_1, where w_i is the linear training-sample
prediction operator for that held sample. Report the term per coordinate/channel;
it is worst-case propagation of 8-bit rounding, not a tolerance fit to residuals.
The half-device knot grid has an exact Nyquist null vector under unit-pixel area
integration and is therefore declined before native capture; one-device spacing
removes that structural null. No per-phase amplitude or held-out profile refit.
Keep the literal area-integrated ramp fit beside as a diagnostic, not a veto.
The end sentinel compares all four zero-shape side-profile raw opaque bands
byte-for-byte and glass band-mean RGB at every coordinate/channel within0.5;
whole-image difference is recorded only diagnostically. Drift makes both axes
UNMEASURED at that scale and admits neither, not an unreachable-actuator claim.
Retain every value, state, profile, rank, condition and failure. A permission
prompt at the first ungranted SCK call is never approved; nobody clicks Allow.
Reachable at both scales admits only that axis's declared seven-run phase cells
(shared zero +3 sizes, each glass/opaque =8 when one axis,14 when both).
Unreachable at either scale omits that axis verbatim; neither axis => zero
phase cells. No window-origin/odd-canvas/transform fallback; no backdrop phase.
