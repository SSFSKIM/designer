W24 G1 (b) — solve-state: what the collapse and the response solve are actually doing, per cell

Read off the calibration page's own publication in the landed 0.12.0 captures
(`web-captures/<profile>/<scene>/report__webgpu.json`, `page.groups[].backdropTone` beside
`page.surfaces[].bounds` and the material patch the run was given), evaluated through the shader's
own arithmetic by `solve-state.py`. The matrix is at `408ad2e`; `--renderer webgpu`.

Columns: `sizeK` the size law's thickness at the surface's span; `toneX` the collapse's argument
(`level + backdropToneSizeBias · sizeK`); `k` the collapse's strength; `alpha` the pre-solve
occlusion; `auth` the W9 response solve's authority; `solve` whether that solve runs at all.

THE FINDING THIS TABLE CARRIES, and it decides G1's mechanism question:

  1. On every `impulse__capsule-button` cell — both schemes, both scales, bare and tinted — the
     collapse is FULL: `k` = 1.0000 exactly (toneX 0.0049, well under `backdropToneLow` 0.02).
  2. On those cells the alpha solve does not run, and it is stood down TWICE over: by `k ≥ 0.995`
     ("at k → 1 the collapse owns the pixel outright") and independently by `auth` = 0 (the
     backdrop's encoded level 0.00375 is below half the dark anchor 0.1104). So the transmission
     cannot have been removed by the solve. It is removed by the collapse's TARGET.
  3. The dark thin structured cells the wave's clause 3 names — `checkerboard__capsule-button` and
     `photo__capsule-button` — have `k` = 0.0000 at both scales in both schemes (toneX 0.219 and
     0.155 against `backdropToneHigh` 0.055). The collapse is not running on them at all, so a
     transmitting collapse cannot reach them; see `g1-findings.md`.
  4. The mechanism's whole reach on this bed is therefore: the four `impulse__capsule-button` cells
     and `impulse__capsule-button__rest-tint-orange` at k = 1, `impulse__rrect-md__rest` at
     k = 0.049, and the `dark-solid__*` capsules at k = 1 where the backdrop is solid and the
     per-pixel sample IS the mean (byte-identical at any constant, which is the fit's stop).
