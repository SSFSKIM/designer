   The light rrect-md over dark-solid reads the same shape (tr 0.069, bl 0.068 against tl 0.279,
   br 0.282, the straight sides 0.24–0.25; landed 0.23 on every corner and 0.32–0.34 on the sides).

   Apple's rim is LIT: brightest where the contour's normal points to the top-left or the
   bottom-right, near zero where it points to the top-right or bottom-left, and 0.7–0.85 of the
   peak on every straight side — the profile of |n · L| (or a root of it) with L on the diagonal,
   symmetric at both ends, plus a small ambient floor (dark: ~0.006 of a 0.042 peak; light: 0.068
   of 0.28). Ours is the same number everywhere the normal is vertical or horizontal and slightly
   less on the arcs — a drawn line.

   Why no instrument saw it: a light on the 45° diagonal projects EQUALLY on all four straight
   sides, so every per-side reader (W21's band, W23's contour span) reads "flat" and L−R = T−B = 0
   exactly as the reference does; the variation lives entirely in the corner arcs, which the
   contour reader excludes by construction and the band reader averages into its "corner
   overshoot" (W23 clause 4 — that miss was this). W22's specular (one-sided Lambert from
   [−0.37, −0.93], ~22° off vertical) was the wrong shape and was rightly fitted to 0; the right
   shape is a symmetric |cos| about the diagonal.
