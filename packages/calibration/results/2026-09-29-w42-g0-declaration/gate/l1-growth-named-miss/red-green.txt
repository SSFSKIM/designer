------------------------

into packages/calibration/.scratch-w42-l1-red/. Each copy seeds the growth of named cells in the
matrix the test loads, and the same cells in the L1 cut beside it (web, error, growth, and the
cut's growthFailures and absoluteMisses), so "re-derives every recorded mean" stays green and the
seed reaches only the growth clause and its owner. A cell's seeded web mean is
n + sign(w - n) (before + g): its error is its W33 baseline error plus the seeded growth g.

dark +0.0138 / +0.0133, 1x / 2x; declaration item l1TintedReceded). Every copy ran through a
