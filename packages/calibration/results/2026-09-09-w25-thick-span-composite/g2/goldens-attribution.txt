WHY IT IS INERT, term by term, and where each statement is pinned.

  the share law        `kDeep` gains `+ lift · sizeThickness(span)` with `lift` 0 at both anchors,
                       so the added term is bit-exactly `0.0 · x` and the clamp around it is the
                       identity on a value already in [floor, 1].
                       Pinned: `thick-span.test.ts` "is inert at the default", and
                       "rides `sizeThickness`, so the thin end cannot move at any lift".

  the level term       the tone response's blend gains `+ levelFar` with `levelFar` resolved to 0 at
                       every span by `sizeToneLevelFar`, so `f` is the smoothstep W9 fitted.
                       Pinned: `thick-span.test.ts` "is inert at the default and exactly 0 at and
                       below `sizeSpanMax`" and "leaves the tone response untouched at every span at
                       or under the knee".

  the along-side field the rim's amplitude gains a factor `max(1 + slope · sizeThick · field, 0)`
                       with `slope` 0 on both variants, which is exactly `1.0`; multiplying a float
                       by exactly 1.0 is bit-exact, so `rw * lit * alongFactor * (…)` is the W24
                       expression to the bit.
                       Pinned: `thick-span.test.ts` "is exactly 1 at the default slope and at every
                       span at or below the band", and `backdrop-tone.test.ts`'s WGSL pin, which was
                       amended to name the factor's new position OUTSIDE W23's amplitude bracket.

THE CAPTURE-LEVEL PROOF, which is stronger than the goldens for this wave's purpose. The goldens are
thirteen synthetic scenes; the bed is the reference's own. `ladder.sh`'s rung `r0` re-rendered the
canonical 1x light and dark beds through `capture:web` with the committed profile documents, into
scratch, on this branch — and every capture that has a canonical sibling in the main checkout's
`web-captures/` is byte-identical to it:

  apple-macos-26.5-1x-light-standard: identical=26 differ=0 no-canonical-capture=0
  apple-macos-26.5-1x-dark-standard:  identical=10 differ=0 no-canonical-capture=16

(The sixteen dark scenes with no canonical capture are the ones the 1x dark profile has no reference
fixture for; nothing was compared against them.) So the resolved material renders the 0.13.0 bed to
the byte, on the reference's own scenes and not only on the golden suite's.
