W25 G2 — rides.txt: the four laws that ride `sizeThickness`, read at every rung of the ladder
====================================================================================================

WHAT THIS ANSWERS. The wave's risk register names one risk above the others: "moving the knee moves
four laws (lens depth, occlusion, inner shadow, tone bias). Mitigation: re-read, not re-fit; the
ladder reads all four at every rung; any that moves against a bound is a stop."

WHAT THE LADDER FOUND, and why it is stronger than a re-read. W25 G0 answered clause 4 in the landed
law's favour — `sizeThickness(short side)` at knee 96 scores r 0.95–0.998 against 0.68–0.96 for
every other candidate on both the width and the level — so THE KNEE DOES NOT MOVE IN THIS WAVE, and
none of W25's three mechanisms touches `sizeSpanMin`, `sizeSpanMax` or any constant the four laws
read. Each of them ADDS a term riding the curve; none re-expresses the curve.

So the four laws are not merely unmoved at the fitted values: they are unmoved at ANY value of
W25's three constants, and that is what the table below measures. It is `predict.mjs rides`, which
evaluates `lensDepthPx`, `sizeOcclusionAlpha`, `sizeShadowDepth` and `backdropToneSizeBias ·
sizeThickness` from the renderer's own module at the landed material (the `(0)` columns) and at a
material with all three of W25's constants set to 1 — a value an order of magnitude outside any fit
this wave produced (the `(1)` columns).

  lensDepth  at an authored thickness of 8 CSS px, `lensDepthPx(8, span)`, in CSS px
  occl       `sizeOcclusionAlpha(tintAlpha, span)` on the regular variant
  shadow     `sizeShadowDepth(shadowDepth, span)` on the regular variant
  bias       `backdropToneSizeBias · sizeThickness(span)`, the collapse's size bias

Every pair is equal to the last digit printed, at every span of the bed. (The inner shadow's column
is flat in SPAN as well, at 0.3500: `sizeShadowGainMax` is 1 on the shipped material, which is the
coupled-by-construction reading `MaterialProfile.sizeShadowGainMax` records — the fixtures cannot
identify it and the constant is held where the objective is flat. That is W2's state and W25 does
not touch it.)

The rendered check is beside this file. `deltae.txt` reads the OKLab ΔE of every non-holdout cell of
the four 1x beds at every rung of the ladder; the thin cells the lens, the occlusion and the inner
shadow are fitted on move by 0.000000 at span 32 and by at most 0.000188 at span 44 — the latter
being the capsule's own 0.0923 of the thickness curve reaching the share law, not any of these four.
