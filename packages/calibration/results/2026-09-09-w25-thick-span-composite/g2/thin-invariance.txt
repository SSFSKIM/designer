TWO KINDS OF ZERO, and the difference is the point.

**Exactly zero, by construction, at span 32.** Every one of the 21 `rrect-sm` cells reads +0.00000 at
every rung — `w9 dark-solid__rrect-sm`, `w21 checkerboard-64__rrect-sm`, `canon-light
photo__rrect-sm`, all of them. `sizeThickness` returns a hard 0 at and below `sizeSpanMin`, and both
the share law and the along-side field are multiplications by it, so no value of either constant can
reach these cells. The level term reaches nothing at or below span 96 for the same kind of reason —
its curve is a smoothstep whose low edge is `sizeSpanMax` — and `fit-level.txt` measured that on 77
rows at a rendered lever of 0.000000 codes per unit. This is X5 discharged by the shape of the
formulas, and `thick-span.test.ts` pins it with `toBe` rather than `toBeCloseTo` in three places.

**Small but not zero, at span 44.** The capsule and the toolbar group take `sizeThickness(44)` =
0.0923 of the share law and of the along-side field. That is the thin end's whole exposure and it is
what the table's non-zero entries are: −0.00016 … +0.00020 across every rung, worst 0.000196. The
sign is not one-way — `canon-light checkerboard__capsule-button` rises 0.00004 at rF while `w21
checkerboard__capsule-button` falls 0.00012 — which is what a term this small looks like against
8-bit quantisation rather than a systematic drift.

WHY THIS IS THE READ AND NOT AN ARGUMENT. The wave's rule is that the thin anchors are "re-read at
every rung", and re-reading a constant is weaker than re-rendering the cell: `sizeScatterFloor`,
`sizeScatterSpanMax` and the three 1x ramp anchors are untouched by W25 — no rung patched one — so a
re-read would return the same number by inspection. What the table above does instead is render the
thin cells at every rung and measure them against Apple's own fixtures. That is what X5 asks for and
it is what `deltae.txt` records.
